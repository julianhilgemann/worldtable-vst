# 06 — UI

Visuals front and centre: one large hero view, the all-sky dome (ADR-0006),
that reacts to both the data and the sound. Since T6.5 everything sits on one
page around it (the owner's arrangement, 2026-11-06); no tabs. Goal: unmistakably its own, not FabFilter-like,
not cookie-cutter.

## Visual identity
- **The palette is physics.** Only the aurora's emission colours on a deep
  night-sky ground. Each colour has a job; iridescence comes from blending
  them, never from extra hues.

  | Token | Hex | Meaning | Job |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `--night` | `#05070D` | night-sky ground | background |
  | `--ink` | `#C9D3E6` | starlight | primary text, hairlines |
  | `--muted` | `#8A96AD` | — | labels, secondary text |
  | `--faint` | `#6E7991` | — | meta, units, idle values (4.6:1, T6.4a) |
  | `--o-green` | `#5CF2A0` | O 557.7 nm, ~110 km | wavetable/curtain body, live state |
  | `--o-red` | `#FF6B6B` | O 630.0 nm, ~250 km | slow, high-altitude modulation, storm |
  | `--n-violet` | `#8F7BFF` | N₂⁺ 427.8 nm, ~100 km | shimmer and highs, Bz |
  | `--day-rim` | faint light blue | stylistic daytime | rim shimmer only |

  (Hexes from the Hero Concepts canvas; tune for contrast ≥ 4.5:1 on text.)
- **Two layers, two materials.** An *organic* layer (the aurora: soft,
  glowing, slow) and an *instrument* layer (hairline traces, small technical
  type, observatory readouts: station, coordinates, UT, Kp). Controls belong
  to the instrument layer and are drawn as thin lines (arcs, hairline faders),
  not chunky knobs.
- **Type:** Instrument Sans (UI) + JetBrains Mono (readouts), bundled with the
  plug-in (SIL OFL), never loaded from the network. Sentence case, no wide tracking,
  sizes 10 · 11 · 12 · 14 · 18 · 48, weights 400 and 500, tabular figures (T6.4a).
- **Light lives in the content.** The dome and the magnetogram glow; the chrome (logo, text,
  dots, controls) does not. Spacing on a 4 px grid (T6.4a).
- **Nothing jitters.** Live numbers sit in fixed-width slots; a changing value never moves its
  neighbours. No separator glyphs: space between fields, commas in prose (T6.4a).
- **References:** historical observatory magnetograms (fine traces on paper
  strip charts), mission-control readouts, all-sky camera frames.

**Work in progress** (owner, 2026-11-06): the arrangement is being iterated, not fixed.
Left and right columns share three equal rows (196 px); the middle column is the dome over the
macros. The dome stays clear: its View switches open from a small button on its left edge. Details (header button) opens Observer, Journey and Data over the page; Escape or a click
outside closes it. 1200 × 800 was the T6.1 size with tabs; the one-page layout needs the extra
room to keep every module at its T6.1 size.
- **Header readouts:** station code and coordinates, date and UT (model time), Kp (red from 5),
  Bz (violet when southward), solar-wind speed, the mode pill (Journey and ×speed; Frozen at 0;
  Live with M5), a hairline output meter.
- **Dome** (centre, 448²): zenith centre, horizon rim with 10° azimuth ticks, compass labels,
  30°/60° rings, magnetic zenith (MZ), sun and moon with phase, bright stars. The corners carry the
  station, sun/moon, the emission legend and the arc/MLT readouts. A faint glow of the sky's
  current emission mix bleeds into the page behind it. Breath → a slow global swell of the
  exposure; the output level → a little glow.
- **Chord:** label, roman numeral, the function ("the awe move · onset → chromatic mediant", in
  the function's colour), the state (The sky writes / MIDI hold / Hold, "next chord waiting"), and
  the voicing view: every resonator voice on a C1–C8 axis, one lane per layer; on a chord change
  the new voices glide in from the nearest old ones while the old fade (voice-leading, visible).
- **Sky:** the phase track with progress, the readouts, and the **event ticker** in words.
- **Macros:** five 62 px hairline arcs with a neutral tick and a live inner ring for what each is
  shaping now (Storm: activity, Breath: the cycle, Glow: violet emission, Space: reverb level,
  Drift: a slow orbit). Drift's arc is the red → violet blend.
- **Magnetogram strip:** the whole replayed day: ΔH measured (green) and modelled (violet),
  coupling (red area) with energy against the cycle threshold, the phase band, onsets ticked by
  kind; the part still ahead is dimmed, now is the red dashed line.
- **Modules:** Harmony (key row, mood, scale, shape, progression, transpose, register, spread,
  detune, drift); Layers with their decay picture; Drive, Phaser, Chorus, Delay, Shimmer reverb,
  Output, each with a live picture that takes the module's spare height (transfer curve, notch
  sweep over the spectrum, chorus voices, delay taps, decay tail with shimmer, meters). View
  switches open from a button on the dome (closed by default). *Motion* and *Data, OSC* (M4, M7) still need a place: open question.

## Interaction
- **Legible:** the event ticker says in words what changed the sound
  ("21:40 UT substorm onset → shift to E major").
- Hovering a mod source highlights its destinations, and vice versa.
- Drag a mod source onto any control → creates a matrix slot.
- Motion is slow, never flashing; `prefers-reduced-motion` calms the dome.
- Every visual target is smoothed (same "no sudden change" rule as sound).

## Rendering
- Dome = the T1.2 sketch's WebGL renderer (`source/ui/dome.js`, ported as a classic script with
  `sky.js`); node arrays arrive as JSON in the 30 Hz telemetry and are eased per frame. Replaces
  the TP.2 ambient background (retired in T6.1).
- Instrument layer = DOM/SVG/canvas, redrawn only when its data changes
  (the POC redraws 12 canvases every frame: a known CPU cost to fix).
- GPU budget: render the dome below CSS resolution if needed, cap ~30–60 fps,
  pause when hidden.

## Mock first
The page runs in a normal browser with a mock backend. The T1.2 Sky Model sketch *is* the mock
(`source/ui/mock.js` imports `prototype/sky/`): serve the repo root and open `/source/ui/`; the
hash picks the moment (`#day=2024-05-10&t=2024-05-11T08:00&speed=24`, `&tab=fx`, `&shot` for
headless screenshots without CSS transitions).

## Exit criteria (M6)
- Dome and instrument layer render in the browser mock and the plug-in. (T6.1)
- Every control maps to a parameter ID in `parameters.yaml`: `WorldtableUICheck` checks that each
  parameter has exactly one `[data-param]` control. (T6.1)
- UI CPU cost measured per view and within budget; owner review notes written.
