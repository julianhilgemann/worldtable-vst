# 06 — UI

One page around the all-sky dome (ADR-0006): the sky's day on top, the sound generators on the
left, the chord on the right, the effects along the bottom, what moves things under the dome.
The editor is a WebView page (`source/ui/`); the same page runs in a browser on a mock backend.

**Design reference:** `spec/mockups/ui-v2.html`, approved by the owner on 2026-11-13. Open it in a
browser, no server needed. It is a picture of the design, not the implementation. Take layout,
sizes, colours, copy and gestures from it, and the code structure from `source/ui/`. Where this
file and the mockup disagree, the mockup wins on looks and this file wins on behaviour.

## 1. Direction
Toward FabFilter: display-led, solid, soft. Each module's picture is its main control. The palette
stays the aurora's, and colour means one thing only. The dome and the keogram are pictures of light;
the chrome is not. Motion is slow and never flashes. UI cost is measured and reported, not capped
(owner, 2026-11-13).

## 2. Five rules
1. **The page follows the signal.** The sky's day on top; the sound generators top left with the
   tone under them; the light in the middle; the chord on the right; the effects along the bottom
   in signal order; what moves things in one block under the dome.
2. **One colour, one meaning.** Red, green and violet are the three emission lines and the three
   resonator bands they play. Everything a person sets is in starlight ink. State (a storm-level
   Kp, a held MIDI chord, a selected button) is said in words, weight and fill, never in a hue.
   Exceptions are pictures of light itself: the dome and the keogram.
3. **What moves a control is drawn on it.** A driven knob carries a second ring: the arc is the
   range its sources can push it, the white dot is the value now. The number under the knob stays
   the value that was set. In a picture the live value is a white dot beside the handle.
4. **Pictures are controls.** Each module's picture is its main control: drag inside it. Knobs
   remain only for values with no place in the picture.
5. **The page explains itself.** A hint line at the bottom says in one sentence what the thing
   under the pointer does. No legends to decode, no tooltips that repeat a name.

## 3. Layout (1350 × 900, fixed 3:2; rows 44 · 92 · 448 · 260 · 28, gaps 7, side margin 10)

```
┌ top bar 44: brand │ ▶/❚❚  10 May 2024 17:30 UT │ 1× 24× 60× 240× 1440× │ day ▾ │ Loop │ … meter ┐
├ day strip 1110 × 92: keogram over the magnetometer,          ┬ Sky 213 × 92: phase, wind/Bz/Kp, ┤
│ onsets, playhead                                             │ energy, activity, last event    │
├ Resonators 435 × 264 ──┬ Dome 448 × 448             ┬ Harmony 433 × 448 ────────────────────────┤
│  bands and crossovers  │                            │  chord, why, the rule, the notes         │
├ EQ 435 × 177 ──────────┤                            │  sounding, key, mood, scale,             │
│  four bands, drive     │                            │  Progression, Transpose, Drone | Play    │
├ Phaser 214 ┬ Chorus 214 ┬ Macros 448 × 112 (one row) ───────┬ Delay 146 ┬ Reverb 175 ┬ Output 98 ┤
│            │            ├ LFO 220 × 141 ┬ Envelope 221 × 141 ┤           │            │           │
└ bottom bar 28: i │ Modulation │ Tour │ S M L │ the hint line │ auto-gain readout ───────────────┘
```
- **Signal order:** Resonators → EQ (with drive) → Phaser → Chorus → Delay → Reverb → Output.
- **Markup:** each module is `div.module.frame#<id>` (`position: relative`) with a `.head` title
  row, a picture, `div.knobs`, and optionally a More popover `div.more.frame#<id>-more` right
  after its title button.
- **Overlays:** the modulation grid covers the dome and Harmony (888 × 448), so the left column
  and the bottom row stay visible and their rings can be watched while editing. Details (the "i"
  chip: Observer, Journey, Sky now, Data) opens over the bottom left; View opens from a button on
  the dome.

## 4. The language
| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ground | `#05070d` | page, behind the dome |
| panel | `linear-gradient(#0f1522, #0b1019)`, 1 px `rgba(201,211,230,.07)`, radius 10, inner top highlight `rgba(255,255,255,.03)` | every module |
| well | `#070a12`, 1 px `rgba(201,211,230,.06)`, radius 7 | every picture |
| ink | `#c9d3e6` (13.4:1); high `#eef2fa`; muted `#8a96ad` (6.8:1); faint `#6e7991` (4.6:1, axis labels only) | text, lines, knobs |
| red, green, violet | `#ff6b6b`, `#5cf2a0`, `#8f7bff` | the three bands only |
| selected | fill `#1b2334`, text ink-high | chips, segments, keys |

- **Type:** Instrument Sans for words, JetBrains Mono for numbers, both bundled (SIL OFL), never
  loaded from the network. Sizes 12 (labels), 13 (module titles, 500), 11–11.5 (values, hints in
  pictures), 17 (clock), 42 (chord). Nothing under 12 px for words outside pictures and the Sky
  block (10.5 px there), 10 px for axis numbers. Sentence case, tabular figures.
- **Nothing jitters:** live numbers sit in fixed-width slots; a changing value never moves its
  neighbours. No separator glyphs: space between fields, commas in prose.
- **Knob:** a solid disc (radial gradient `#232c40` → `#111724`, rim `rgba(201,211,230,.22)`), a
  2 px pointer, a 2.5 px value arc outside the disc on a faint track, label and value underneath.
  Disc 28 px (footprint 48), macros 40 px. Bipolar values draw the arc from the centre. The
  modulation ring sits 8 px outside the disc. Band knobs take their band's colour, all others ink.
- **Handle in a picture:** a 5.5–8 px ring (ink-high stroke, ground fill). Crossover grips are
  8 × 22 px pills. A dragged or hot handle shows its value in a small label or in the title row.
- **Chip / segment:** height 22–28, radius 5–7; selected state as in the table.
- **Hit sizes:** nothing a pointer must hit is smaller than 24 × 24 px at Medium (20 at Small),
  bar the drive bars and grid dots, whose hit areas are padded to that.
- **Motion:** every drawn value eases (`CLAUDE.md` rule 4); `prefers-reduced-motion` calms the
  dome and cuts the tour's spotlight instead of gliding it.

## 5. Parameter binding (keeps `WorldtableUICheck` honest)
Every parameter has **exactly one** element with `data-param="<id>"`. It is focusable, has a role
(`slider`, `switch`, `radiogroup`), an `aria-valuetext`, and moves with the arrow keys (Shift for
fine, Home/End, Enter to type a value, double-click or Backspace for the default). For a parameter
set inside a picture, that element is the handle's proxy: a small focusable element placed on the
handle, so the focus ring appears there. A second view of a value (the band row's Level knob)
carries `data-param-view`. Parameters without a visible control live in the module's **More**
popover (click the module title; Escape or a click outside closes it); they still count. Every
`data-param` element and every picture has a `data-hint` sentence (≤ 90 characters, says what it
does to the sound, never repeats the name alone). The page changes a parameter only through
`UI.commit` or a begin/set/end gesture (JUCE asserts on nested gestures).

## 6. The modules
- **Top bar (the Journey transport):** run/freeze (the icon swaps), the clock (`10 May 2024
  17:30 UT`, fixed width), a five-way speed segment (1×, 24×, 60×, 240×, 1440×), the day menu
  (the recorded days; "Live sky" listed, disabled until the data milestone), a Loop chip, the
  output meter. Native `journeySet({ frozen?, speed?, loop?, day? })`; the state is the plug-in's
  `journey` child. The space bar is not bound (hosts use it).
- **Day strip** (1110 × 92): a keogram of the replayed day, the dome's oval shader along the
  north–south meridian (rows "north", "overhead", "south"), over the measured ΔH ("field") on a
  zero line; hour marks every 6 h, the date at midnight; onsets as carets on the top edge (hollow
  for pseudo-breakups, sized by strength); what is still to come dimmed; the playhead a white
  line with a cap. Hover reads the moment into the hint line, a click jumps there, a drag scrubs
  (native `seek`, applied click-free at the next block); arrow keys step 10 minutes. The keogram's
  constants sit together in `KEO` (`views.js`), tuned by eye.
- **Sky** (213 × 92): the phase with its progress, wind, Bz and Kp in one line, energy against the
  release threshold, activity, the last event in words. Details holds the full numbers.
- **Resonators:** the bands on a MIDI 20 … 99 axis (C1 … C7) over the output spectrum: each band a
  filled shape between its edges, as high as its level; five grips (floor, three crossovers,
  ceiling) dragged sideways in semitones (Shift moves all five); a band dragged up or down sets its
  level, a click selects it. One line per note ringing, as high as that note's excitation. The
  band row under it belongs to the selection (name, Tones, Shift, Level, Decay); Detune, Drift and
  Width always. Low is ink; the others their colours.
- **EQ:** log frequency 20 Hz … 20 kHz, ±12 dB. Four numbered dots (three bells, the cut on its
  −3 dB point): sideways frequency, up and down gain, scroll a bell's width; a drive bar under each
  in the drive lane. Behind, the source spectrum shaped by the curve; the curve as set thin, the
  curve now (with its modulation) bright; the reach of whatever moves the cut on the top edge,
  read only. The title row reads the selected band out. The page's response is `fx/Eq.h`'s
  (`UI.eqDb`; a unit test compares them). All ink.
- **Dome** (448 × 448): the sketch's renderer (§9), its corner readouts (station, sun and moon,
  emission legend, arc and MLT) and its View button. A faint glow of the sky's emission mix
  bleeds into the page behind it.
- **Harmony:** the state line and the Drone | Play switch; the chord (42 px), its roman numeral
  and the "why" line (`<function>, because <the sky's phase in words>`); the rule row (Quiet →
  home, Growth → tension, Onset → the awe move, Recovery → borrowed; the current one filled); a
  keyboard strip C1–C7 with the notes sounding in their band's colour (held notes in Play mode);
  key, mood, scale, then Progression and Transpose. In Play mode Attack, Release, Velocity and
  Drone take the place of mood and scale.
- **Effects:** each picture shows what the effect does to the sound and sets its main values.
  Phaser: the source spectrum in, the same through the phaser's exact response filled (notches
  dotted, the sweep range on the bottom edge); drag for depth and feedback; Stages in the title.
  Chorus: the dry line, the band the voices swing in, three voices scrolling to a "now" marker;
  drag for rate. Delay: the dry hit and each echo as a bar (ping-pong alternates), a handle on the
  first echo for time and feedback. Reverb: the tail's level over log time with the shimmer inside
  it and the live level; drag for decay. Output: the meters and the limiter's ceiling, dragged.
  Knobs: Phaser Rate, Feed, Mix; Chorus Rate, Depth, Mix; Delay Tone, Mix; Reverb Size, Shimmer,
  Mix; Output Out. The rest sit under More.
- **The motion block** (under the dome): the Macros row (Storm, Breath, Glow, Space, Drift at
  40 px; "point at one to see what it moves"; the LFO and envelope parameters under its More),
  the LFO panel (1 | 2, four shapes, the wave scrolling under a fixed "now" marker with a dot at
  the value and what still comes dimmer, Rate; drag sideways for the rate) and the Envelope panel
  (a trigger chip: Onset, Note, Fire only; Fire; the shape with three points to drag, the held
  stretch shaded, a dot running along it when it fires, the four times read out). Each picture
  says in its top-left corner what its source moves.

## 7. Modulation
Every routing is one grid of sources × destinations, each depth −1 … +1, 0 = no route
(`source/dsp/mod/ModMatrix.h`; `spec/05-sound-engine.md` §6). The page shares it through
`UI.mod` (`mod.js`): `info` (the `modulation` event: sources, destinations, targets, depths; also
native `modGet()`), `tel` (telemetry `mod`: the sources, every target's value and range, the LFOs,
the envelope), `set` (native `modSet`), `routesOf`, `movedBy`, `on`, `destName`, `amountText`.
Order, labels and modules come from the payload; the page hard-codes none of them.
- **Sources:** Macros (Storm, Breath, Glow, Space, Drift), Sky (Activity, Onset), Motion (LFO 1,
  LFO 2, Envelope). **Destinations** (21): Resonators (the four levels, Detune, Drift, Width,
  Strike: the exciter, no knob); EQ (gains of bands 1–3, the cut, Drive: all four drives);
  Phaser mix; Chorus mix; Delay feedback and mix; Reverb decay, shimmer and mix; Progression.
- **Rings** (rule 3): every knob with a route shows the range and the live dot from telemetry; a
  value past the knob's top (the levels to 2, Detune, Drift) runs the ring past the track's end.
  In the resonator and EQ pictures the live value is a white dot beside the handle.
- **Pointing at a source** (a macro, the LFO panel, the envelope panel, a grid row) lights the
  knobs it moves; the hint line lists them ("Space: … Moves: Delay mix, Reverb mix.").
- **Context menu on a control:** "Modulate with ▸" (a route at +50 %) and the control's routes,
  each a row that clears it.
- **The grid** (`grid.js`; the Modulation button on the bottom bar or the context menu): an
  overlay over the dome and Harmony, drawn on one canvas. Sources as rows in their three groups,
  each with a small live meter under its name; destinations as columns under their module's name
  (a module with one column has no heading); the band levels' labels in their band's colour,
  everything else ink. A dot's size is the depth; filled pushes up, hollow pushes down. Drag a dot
  up or down to set it (70 px for the full range), double-click to clear. With the grid focused
  the arrows move between cells, + and − (or Page Up and Page Down; Shift finer) set the depth,
  Backspace clears. The row and column under the pointer are lit and the hint line reads the
  route ("Breath moves Violet level by −50 %."). Escape or the Modulation button closes it.

## 8. The window: sizes, menu, tour
- **Sizes:** Small 85 % (1148 × 765), Medium 100 % (1350 × 900), Large 125 % (1688 × 1125), the
  same page scaled. Native `setUiSize(0|1|2)`; the choice is per computer (`Worldtable.settings`
  in the user's application-data folder, key `uiSize`; `$WORLDTABLE_SETTINGS_FILE` overrides
  it). Default Medium; Small when the display's usable area is under Medium plus 80 px.
- **Context menu** (the page's own; the web view's never shows): on the background the sizes, the
  modulation grid, the welcome tour and About; on a control its hint dimmed, Reset to default,
  Enter value…, and Modulate with ▸ with its routes. Clamped to the stage; Escape, a click
  outside or a choice closes it. Everything in it is reachable without a right-click.
- **Welcome tour:** five steps, each a spotlight on a real region with a card (the day strip, the
  dome, Harmony, the left column, the motion block). The page stays live under it. Shown once per
  computer, 1.5 s after the first editor opens (`tourSeen`); reopened from the bottom bar and the
  menu; Escape skips.
- **Hint line:** the bottom bar's centre; the `data-hint` of whatever is under the pointer or in
  focus, or a picture's own sentence for the spot under the pointer.

## 9. Rendering
- **Dome:** the T1.2 sketch's WebGL renderer (`dome.js` with `sky.js`); node arrays arrive in the
  30 Hz telemetry and are eased per frame (critically damped, τ 85 ms × 2) and smoothed along the
  chain; the curtain is a Catmull-Rom spline at 8 columns per node and 24 rows; the fine rays are
  drawn per pixel; bloom has four levels with a dither against banding. Up to 60 fps; the
  instrument pictures at 30.
- **WebKit:** an overlay over the dome's WebGL canvas needs its own compositing layer
  (`transform: translateZ(0)`), or it is clickable but not painted. Never put a compositing layer
  under page content. An absolutely positioned popover is sized from its children's widths.
- **Canvases** are never sized by their own backing store: `UI.fit` caps at 2048 CSS px and
  min(3, dpr × zoom); flex-item canvases take `width: 0; min-width: 0` or sit absolutely in a well.
  Check sizes in the real editor (`WorldtableUICheck --eval`), not only in Chrome.
- **Cost:** no markup is rewritten at 30 Hz; no CSS variables are set on `#stage` or other large
  ancestors; pictures redraw when their data changes.

## 10. Mock first, and the check
- The page runs in a normal browser: serve the repo root and open `/source/ui/`. The T1.2 sketch
  is the mock (`mock.js` imports `prototype/sky/`; `mod.js` plays the modulation engine). Review
  hashes: `#day=…&t=…&speed=…`, `#set=<id>:<v>,…`, `#more=<module>`, `#details`, `#view`,
  `#tour=N`, `#menu`, `#menu=<param>`, `#about`, `#grid`, `#grid=<row>,<col>`,
  `#routes=<src>><dst>:<depth>,…`, and `&shot` (no CSS transitions, no tour) for headless
  screenshots.
- `WorldtableUICheck` opens the real editor and checks the bridge end to end: every parameter has
  exactly one `[data-param]` (0 unbound), its hint, role and keys; the pictures' drags reach the
  processor; the rings equal the engine's telemetry; the transport, the day strip, the sizes, the
  menu, the tour and the grid work; every overlay has its compositing layer.
