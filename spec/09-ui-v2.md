## 2. Five rules
1. **The page follows the signal.** The sky's day on top; the sound generators top left with the
   tone under them; the light in the middle; the chord on the right; the effects along the bottom
   in signal order; what moves things in one block under the dome.
2. **One colour, one meaning.** Red, green and violet are the three emission lines and the three
   resonator bands they play. Everything a person sets is in starlight ink. State (a storm-level
   Kp, a held MIDI chord, a selected button) is said in words, weight and fill, never in a hue.
   Exceptions are pictures of light itself: the dome and the keogram.
3. **What moves a control is drawn on it.** A driven knob carries a second ring: the arc is the
   range its sources can push it, the white dot is the value now. The number under the knob stays
   the value that was set. In a picture the live value is a white dot beside the handle.
4. **Pictures are controls.** Each module's picture is its main control: drag inside it. Knobs
   remain only for values with no place in the picture.
5. **The page explains itself.** A hint line at the bottom says in one sentence what the thing
   under the pointer does. No legends to decode, no tooltips that repeat a name.

## 4. The language (toward FabFilter: display-led, solid, soft; the palette stays the aurora's)
| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ground | `#05070d` | page, behind the dome |
| panel | `linear-gradient(#0f1522, #0b1019)`, 1 px `rgba(201,211,230,.07)`, radius 10, inner top highlight `rgba(255,255,255,.03)` | every module (replaces the corner-tick frame) |
| well | `#070a12`, 1 px `rgba(201,211,230,.06)`, radius 7 | every picture |
| ink | `#c9d3e6` (13.4:1); high `#eef2fa`; muted `#8a96ad` (6.8:1); faint `#6e7991` (4.6:1, axis labels only) | text, lines, knobs |
| red, green, violet | `#ff6b6b`, `#5cf2a0`, `#8f7bff` | the three bands only |
| selected | fill `#1b2334`, text ink-high | chips, segments, keys |

- **Type:** Instrument Sans for words, JetBrains Mono for numbers. Sizes 12 (labels), 13 (module
  titles, 500), 11–11.5 (values, hints in pictures), 17 (clock), 42 (chord). Nothing under 12 px
  for words outside pictures and the Sky block (10.5 px there), 10 px for axis numbers. Sentence case.
- **Knob:** a solid disc (radial gradient `#232c40` → `#111724`, rim `rgba(201,211,230,.22)`), a
  2 px pointer, a 2.5 px value arc outside the disc on a faint track, label and value underneath.
  Disc 28 px (footprint 48), macros 40 px. Bipolar values draw the arc from the centre. The
  modulation ring sits 8 px outside the disc. Band knobs take their band's colour, all others ink.
- **Handle in a picture:** a 5.5–8 px ring (ink-high stroke, ground fill). Crossover grips are
  8 × 22 px pills. A dragged or hot handle shows its value in a small label or in the title row.
- **Chip / segment:** height 22–28, radius 5–7; selected state as in the table.
- **Sizes:** nothing a pointer must hit is smaller than 24 × 24 px at Medium (20 at Small), bar
  the drive bars and grid dots, whose hit areas are padded to that.
- **Motion:** unchanged rules (`CLAUDE.md` rule 4): every drawn value eases; reduced motion calms.

## 5. Parameter binding (keeps `WorldtableUICheck` honest)
Every parameter has **exactly one** element with `data-param="<id>"`. It is focusable, has a role
(`slider`, `switch`, `radiogroup`), an `aria-valuetext`, and moves with the arrow keys (Shift for
fine, Home/End, Enter to type a value, double-click or Backspace for the default). For a parameter
set inside a picture, that element is the handle's proxy: a small focusable element placed on the
handle, so the focus ring appears there. Parameters without a visible control live in the module's
**More** popover (click the module title; Escape or a click outside closes it); they still count.
Every `data-param` element and every picture has a `data-hint` sentence (≤ 90 characters, says
what it does to the sound, never repeats the name alone).

## 6. What does not change
- The dome renderer (`source/ui/dome.js`), its corner readouts and its View button.
- The sound, except where a task says so (T3.8, T3.9, T4.2).
- Bundled fonts, the mock-first workflow (`source/ui/mock.js`), the 30 Hz telemetry.
- The WebKit rules from `06-ui.md` §Rendering: overlays over a WebGL canvas need their own
  compositing layer (`transform: translateZ(0)`); a canvas is never sized by its own backing store
  (`UI.fit`, `width: 0; min-width: 0` on flex items); check sizes in the real editor
  (`WorldtableUICheck --eval`), not only in Chrome.
- UI cost is **measured and reported** per task (WebContent CPU, soak), not capped (owner).
