# T6.7 — UI v2 I: the language and the parts
**Milestone:** M6   **Depends on:** T6.6   **Branch:** feat/t6.7-ui2-language
**Status:** approved by the owner, 2026-11-13.   **Read first:** `spec/09-ui-v2.md` §2, §4, §5; `spec/mockups/ui-v2.html`

## Goal
Restyle the page in the v2 language **without moving any module** (T6.8 moves them). After this
task the current layout looks like the mockup's materials: soft panels, solid knobs, ink for
everything a person sets, a hint line that explains each control, and every control usable from
the keyboard. The sound does not change.

## Design
- **Tokens and panels** (`style.css`): the table in `09` §4. `.frame` (corner ticks) becomes the
  panel; pictures sit in wells. Type sizes per `09` §4: no word under 12 px.
- **Knob v2** (`widgets.js`): the anatomy in `09` §4 and the mockup's `knob()`. Keep the existing
  interaction (vertical drag, Shift fine, wheel, double-click default). Add: `tabindex`,
  `role="slider"`, `aria-valuemin/max/now/valuetext`, arrow keys (2 % of the range, Shift 0.4 %),
  Home/End, Backspace = default, Enter = type a value (a small inline field; Escape cancels).
  Expose `setMod(lo, hi, now)` / `clearMod()` for the ring (T4.2 feeds it; until then the macros'
  hard-wired targets use it, see below).
- **Colour rule:** `data-color` is `red|green|violet` only on controls that act on one band (the
  level and decay of red, green, violet; Low stays ink). Every other knob is ink. Selected
  segments, toggles, the Play pill and the Journey pill use the "selected" fill, not green or
  violet. The header's Kp and Bz stop changing colour: Kp ≥ 5 reads `Kp 7.7 storm`, a southward Bz
  reads `south` after the unit. Phase track, chord function and ticker emphasis become ink + weight.
- **Macro rings now:** the five macros are hard-wired (`dsp/Instrument.cpp`: Storm → exciter gain
  0.4 + 1.2·m and Progression; Glow → violet level, reverb shimmer ×(0.5 + m); Space → delay mix,
  reverb mix ×(0.5 + m); Drift → detune, drift ×(0.5 + m)). Draw those rings on the target knobs
  from the same formulas (range = the macro at 0 and 1, dot = now), and light the targets while a
  macro is hovered or focused. Each macro's hint sentence names what it moves.
- **Hint line:** a 28 px bottom bar (the stage's grid gains a row; the dome keeps its size, the
  three side rows give up the height). Every `[data-param]` and every picture gets `data-hint`;
  `title` attributes and the floating tooltip go. Sentences: say what it does to the sound.
- **Segments, toggles, key row:** restyle; make them real `button`s in a `radiogroup` / `switch`
  with arrow-key movement and a visible focus ring (2 px ink-high, 2 px offset).

**What changed**
- `style.css` rewritten on the `09` §4 tokens: every module is a soft panel (`.frame`: gradient
  `#0f1522 → #0b1019`, 1 px `rgba(201,211,230,.07)`, radius 10, inset top highlight); every picture is a
  well (`#070a12`, radius 7: `canvas.view`, `#cv-layers`, `#cv-voicing`, `#cv-keys`, and `#mag` as a whole).
  Words in Instrument Sans (12, titles 13/500), numbers in JetBrains Mono (values 11, clock 17, chord 42).
- Knob v2 (`widgets.js`, the mockup's `knob()`): disc 28 (macros 40) with the `#kg` radial gradient, a
  2 px pointer, a 2.5 px value arc at R + 3.5 on a track, bipolar arcs from the centre (Transpose,
  Register, Storm/Glow/Space/Drift), label 12 sans and value 11 mono under it. `tabindex`, `role=slider`,
  `aria-valuemin/max/now/valuetext`, arrows 2 % (Shift 0.4 %, ints one step), PageUp/Down 10 %,
  Home/End, Backspace/Delete = default, Enter = a small inline field (Enter sets, Escape and blur
  cancel; it reads what the knob shows: percent, kHz, ms/s, and the words neutral, hold, follow sky,
  open, off, still, fixed, −∞). API for T4.2: `knob.setMod(lo, hi, now)` in **parameter units** (as
  `WT.get` returns; clamped to the knob's range for drawing), `knob.clearMod()`, `knob.mod` (getter),
  `knob.setLit(on)`. All knobs are in `UI.knobs[id]`.
- Segments, the key row and `low_octave`/`flt_type` are `role=radiogroup` of `role=radio` buttons
  (roving tabindex; arrows wrap, Home/End, Backspace = default). Ping-pong is a chip and Play the
  header button, both `role=switch` (Space/Enter click natively, arrows set on/off). The dome's View
  toggles became `button role=switch`. Focus ring: 2 px ink-high, 2 px offset.
- One host gesture per parameter at a time (`gesture(id)` in `widgets.js`, `UI.commit(id, v)`): a
  key or wheel run is one gesture, ended 400 ms after the last step; a drag, click or typed value ends a
  pending run first. JUCE asserts on a nested `beginChangeGesture` (the old wheel + double-click could).
- Colour rule: only the six band knobs carry `data-color`; Low and everything else is ink (arc
  `#dfe6f3`). Effect pictures (filter, drive, phaser, chorus, delay, reverb, output, header meter) are
  drawn in ink / ink-high / white. Selected segments, the Play and Journey pills, chips and the phase
  track use the selected fill `#1b2334` + ink-high (+ weight 500 where it is state). Header: `Kp 7.7
  storm`, `Bz −9.1 nT south` (words in fixed 5-character slots so nothing jumps). Chord "why", ticker
  emphasis, the chord pill: ink-high + weight.
- Hint line: `#hintbar`, 28 px, the stage's last row. Every `[data-param]` (58), every canvas and
  the dome carry `data-hint` (≤ 90 characters, `UI.HINT` in `widgets.js` for parameters, the
  pictures' sentences in `index.html`); pointer-over and keyboard focus both drive it. All `title`
  attributes and the floating tooltip (`UI.showTip`) are gone.
- Macro rings (`app.js`, `MACROS`/`updateRings`, 30 Hz): from the engine's formulas —
  Storm → Progression × (0.4 + 1.2 m) (clamped as `Instrument::applyControls`); Glow → Violet level and
  Shimmer × (0.5 + m); Space → Delay mix and Reverb mix × (0.5 + m); Drift → Detune and Drift × (0.5 + m);
  Breath → red and green levels × (1 − 0.35 d c), violet also × (1 − 0.15 d c) (`SkySound.cpp`, c = the
  breath cycle from telemetry). Arc = the target with the macro at 0 and 1 (Breath also at the top of
  its swell), dot = now; several macros on one knob multiply (Violet: Glow × Breath). Hover or focus on
  a macro lights its targets; its hint ends "Moves: …".
- Layout, no module moved: stage rows 48 · 64 · 152 · 1fr become **44 · 64 · 164 · 1fr · 28** (gaps 8).
  The header lost 4 px (the mockup's 44), the hint line took 28, the Harmony/Sky row *gained* 12 px (its
  24 px segments and solid knobs did not fit 152: four choice rows + title need ~155), so the main row is
  568 (was 604): the dome keeps 448, Macros is 112 (the mockup's height, its title moved to the panel's
  side as in the mockup), and the side columns' three rows each gave up ~12 px (Layers gets flex 1.18 for
  its two knob rows). The brand's subtitle "a magnetosphere drone" went (mockup).

**Where brief, mockup and task disagreed or were silent (my calls)**
1. The brand mark keeps its green and red strokes (the mockup has them). It is the one DOM exception
   beyond the criterion's list; UiCheck's colour probe exempts `.brand`.
2. Type: words ≥ 12 everywhere outside pictures (the mockup's 11.5 subtitles and segments became 12).
   Knob values and ticker timestamps are 11 px mono (`09` §4: "11–11.5 values"). The dome's corner
   readouts stay 10 px (`09` §6: unchanged). The magnetogram's legend stays 10 px mono: five lanes in
   64 px leave no room, and T6.10 replaces the picture. Axis numbers are 10 px everywhere.
3. Breath is not in the task's list of hard-wired macros but is one (above), so it has rings too.
4. Storm's other target, the exciter gain, has no knob: its ring is on Progression only; the hint says
   "Moves: the strike, Progression".
5. The macros lost their T6.1 "live" rings (activity, breath, wet level, drift dot): in v2 a ring means
   "something moves this knob", and a macro is a source.
6. Hidden controls are not focusable: the Play controls (attack, release, velocity, drone) take focus
   only in Play mode, when they are shown. UiCheck switches Play on (transitions held) to test them.
7. The chord's "why" uses the mockup's copy ("tension, because the sky is in growth").
