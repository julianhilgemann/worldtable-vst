# T6.6 — The filter's picture, jumping in the day, a smoother aurora
**Milestone:** M6   **Depends on:** T3.7, T6.5   **Branch:** feat/t6.6-filter-seek-dome

Asked for by the owner on 2026-11-09, after T3.7:
> "can we add an animation for the filter, like that slope that moves with the parameters […]
> I would like to be able to click into the timeline view and jump to that place in time instead
> of only being able to listen to the action passively. And finally, I would like the aurora
> animation to be smoother, in a eyecandy way instead of being jagged. Smoother, higher
> resolution, more and better bloom, in a way to signal higher visual fidelity and quality. Also
> the jagged movements are too jagged, overall needs to be smoother"

## Goal
1. **Filter:** the response slope glides with every parameter (cutoff, resonance, type, the sky's
   opening) instead of jumping, with a lit body under the curve and the sky's reach visible.
2. **Seek:** a click on the magnetogram jumps the Journey to that moment; hovering shows where.
   The sound follows without a jolt; the chords move to what the sky is doing there.
3. **Aurora:** smoother in shape and motion, finer, with a fuller bloom, without losing the
   sketch's character (spec/06: light lives in the dome).

## Design
- **Seek path:** page → native function `seek(utc)` → `SkyRuntime::seek` (exists, applied at the
  next block, bounded). `SkyModel` counts seeks; `SkySound` sees a seek after its first tick and
  (a) re-bases the exciter's brightness so the jump is not read as a rising sky (it would swell
  the bank), (b) tells the harmony the phase it landed in (an onset phase as an onset at the last
  onset's strength), so the progression moves there. Colours already slew 2 s, pan 0.5 s.
  Seeks before the first tick (the render tool, the goldens) change nothing.
- **Dome:** node data eased by a critically damped second-order follower (no velocity kinks at
  each 30 Hz frame) and lightly smoothed along the chain; the curtain built from a Catmull-Rom
  spline at 8 columns per node and 24 rows (was linear, 4 × 14); rays sampled along the chain,
  not per column, plus a fine third octave; the red top and the curtain ends faded instead of
  cut. Bloom in four levels (full, ½, ¼, ⅛ resolution) with wider kernels, a gentler shoulder in
  the tone curve and a dither against banding. The dome draws at 60 fps, the rest of the page
  stays at 30.
- **Filter view:** the drawn cutoff, resonance and type weights ease (~120 ms), the area under the
  slope is lit violet, the sky's reach (the cutoff at full activity) shows as a faint band.

## Done
- **Found on the way (the real reason the filter showed no slope):** in the plug-in, on Retina,
  the T3.7 filter canvas grew without bound (67 million px wide: its flex item's minimum width was
  its own backing store, which `UI.fit` set to CSS width × 2 on every redraw), so it drew nothing
  and kept the page busy. `#cv-filter` now has `width: 0; min-width: 0`; `UI.fit` caps at 2048 CSS
  px. The browser mock (Chrome at 1×) never showed it.
- **Filter picture:** eased slope (log cutoff, resonance, type as a crossfade, τ 120 ms), lit
  passband, the knob's cutoff (green tick) and the sky's reach (green band) on the axis.
- **Seek:** native function `seek(utc)` in the editor → `SkyRuntime::seek`; `SkyModel::seeks()`
  counts them; `SkySound` re-bases the exciter (`Exciter::rebase`) and tells the harmony the landing
  phase (onset phases as an onset at the last onset's strength, ≥ 0.3) on a seek after its first
  tick. The magnetogram shows a hover cursor ("11 May 08:00 UT, recovery, click to jump") and seeks
  on click; the ticker logs the jump. The mock seeks the sketch's model the same way
  (`WT_MOCK_NATIVE`).
- **Dome** (`source/ui/dome.js`, now departs from the sketch, marked T6.6): second-order node ease
  (τ 85 ms × 2, longitudes and MLT the short way round), chain smoothing for the picture
  (position 9-tap, colour 3-tap), Catmull-Rom curtain at 8 × 24 (was linear 4 × 14), fine rays
  per pixel in the fragment shader (two octaves, their drift wrapped on the noise period in
  double precision), red faded at the top, the chain's ends faded, edge-on brightening ×2.9 (was
  ×4), four-level bloom, bright cores whitened slightly, dither. The vertex loop dropped
  `domeXY` (two trig calls and an object per vertex). Draws at up to 60 fps.
- **Tools:** `WorldtableUICheck --eval '<js>'` (with `--soak`: runs again at the end, 20 s
  timeout) probes the real editor; the page keeps `__wtState.domeMs` / `meshMs` (eased cost per
  frame) and `__wtState.domeHz` (cap) for the T6.4 cost pass.

## Open questions
- The curtain still bends sharply where it passes close to the observer: that's perspective on a
  real bend of the footprint, not a rendering artefact; smoothing it more would move the aurora.
- Seek is click-only; dragging to scrub, and Journey day/speed choice, stay with the autopilot (T4.4).
- The UI's own cost (~34–37 % of a core in WebContent, most of it not the dome) is the T6.4 cost
  pass: the 30 Hz telemetry JSON and the instrument pictures are the next suspects.
