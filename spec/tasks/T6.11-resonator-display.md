# T6.11 — The resonator display
**Milestone:** M6   **Depends on:** T3.8, T6.8   **Branch:** feat/t6.11-resonator-display
**Status:** approved by the owner, 2026-11-13.   **Read first:** `spec/09-ui-v2.md` §2, §4, §5; mockup `#res`, `drawRes`, the band row under it

Owner, 2026-11-13: the resonator settings looked "rather meh"; crossovers "that change the bands of
colors … if dragged, one band gets elongated, while the other one gets shorter."

## Goal
One display for the resonators (top left, 435 × 264), on a pitch axis: the four bands side by side in their colours, the
crossovers as grips you drag, the notes ringing as lines inside the bands. It replaces the Layers
decay picture and absorbs what the voicing lanes showed. The sound does not change.

## Design
- **Axis:** MIDI 20 … 99 left to right, octave lines and labels C1 … C7. Behind everything, the
  output spectrum (`tel.spectrum`) mapped onto the same axis, faint, in ink.
- **Bands:** each band is a filled shape from its lower to its upper edge; its height is its
  level (`low_level`, `red_level`, `green_level`, `violet_level`; full level reaches 86 % of the
  picture's height). Neighbours fade into each other across the blend (`band_blend`). The band's
  name sits at its top left; a small pill on its top edge shows it can be dragged. The selected
  band is drawn stronger. Low is ink; the others their colours.
- **Grips:** five vertical hairlines with an 8 × 22 px pill: floor, three crossovers, ceiling
  (`band_floor`, `band_x1`, `band_x2`, `band_x3`, `band_ceiling`). Dragging one moves it in
  semitones between its neighbours' minimum widths (T3.8). While hot or dragged it shows the note
  and its frequency ("D3  147 Hz"). Shift-drag on a crossover moves all five together (that is
  what Register was).
- **Notes ringing:** for each band, one line per distinct note in `tel.chord.notes[layer]`, from
  the baseline up to the band's level × that note's excitation. The excitation is real: voice *i*
  is curtain node *i* (`voicesPerNode` 1), so sum the node's emission (`tel.dome.red/green/violet`)
  over the voices on that note and normalise by the layer's maximum. The low band has one line at
  `tel.low.note`, height `tel.low.level`. Lines ease (≈ 120 ms). On a chord change the lines
  glide from the nearest old note, as the voicing view does today.
- **Gestures:** drag a grip sideways; drag a band up or down for its level; click a band to select
  it. The band row under the picture belongs to the selection: its name with a colour swatch, a
  Tones chip (`*_tones`; disabled for Low, which rings the root), a Shift chip (`*_shift`), Level
  and Decay knobs in the band's colour. To the right, always: Detune, Drift, Width.
- **Binding** (`09` §5): each grip and each band's level has one focusable proxy element with
  `data-param` on the picture (arrow keys move it by a semitone or 2 %). The Level knob in the band
  row shows the selected band's level but is not a second `data-param`: it carries
  `data-param-view`. Decay, Tones and Shift for all bands exist once each (the row swaps which
  ones are visible).
- **Hints** for the grips, the bands (with range and tones in words, as the mockup does) and the
  empty picture.
- **Keyboard strip in Harmony** (T6.8) takes its notes and colours from the same data.

## Notes (agent, 2026-11-13)
- **Where:** `source/ui/resonators.js` (new; `UI.ResonatorDisplay(root)`), wired in `app.js` as
  `UI.res` and drawn from the render loop with the telemetry. Markup between the `T6.11` comments in
  `#resonators`; CSS between the `T6.11` comments in `style.css`. `LayersView` is gone from `views.js`.
- **Elements:** `canvas#cv-res.res-view` in `.well.res-well`; the proxies are added into that well:
  `.proxy.grip[data-param=band_floor|band_x1|band_x2|band_x3|band_ceiling]` and
  `.proxy.level[data-param=low_level|red_level|green_level|violet_level]` (each level proxy has
  `aria-controls="bn-<band>"`, the band row it opens). The band row is `.bandrow` with one
  `.bn-band#bn-low|red|green|violet` per band (only the selected one shown) and `.res-globals`
  (Detune, Drift, Width). The row's Level knobs carry `data-param-view` (not counted), Decay,
  Tones and Shift are each band's single binding.
- **New widgets** (`widgets.js`): `UI.Proxy(host, id, {cls, limits})`, a picture handle's binding
  (role slider, value text, hint, keys, Enter to type, double-click default; the picture does the
  pointer); `UI.Chip(host, id, {label, fmt, cycle})`, a chip that steps through values; `UI.begin` /
  `UI.end` for drags in a picture (they end a pending key run first); `Knob` takes `view: true`;
  `UI.showHint(node)` (app.js) for pictures whose hint follows the pointer. T6.14 can reuse Proxy.
- **Gestures:** a grip is caught within 8 px; it moves in semitones between its neighbours' minimum
  widths (the engine's `clampEdges`, mirrored in `clampEdges`/`limitsOf`) and on the axis (the
  ceiling stops at 99 although its parameter reaches 108). Shift at the press moves all five by
  the same step. A band: press selects, vertical drag sets its level (Shift fine). Double-click: the
  default. The bands and grips are drawn from the parameters (they follow the pointer at once); the
  lines from `tel.chord.notes` (they move when the engine re-voices, ~300 ms after the last move).
- **Lines:** one per distinct note and band; height = the band's live level (its macro ring's "now")
  × the note's summed node emission / the band's strongest note; ease 120 ms; on a chord change new
  lines glide (3.2 s) from the nearest old note of their band, old ones fade (0.6 s). The low line
  sits at `tel.low.note`, `tel.low.level` high. `UI.res.lines()` returns `[band, note, height]`.
- **Checked in the real editor** (`--eval`): lines = `tel.chord.notes` exactly (red 38 45 50,
  green 48 … 74, violet 72 79 84, low 26); `#cv-res` 407 × 135 CSS, 814 × 270 backing at dpr 2;
  UiCheck drags x1 36 → 40 with pointer events and C++ reads 40.
- **UiCheck:** `#cv-layers` → `#cv-res, .bn-sw` in the colour rule's exemptions; More params
  ≥ 10 (12 now, after T6.12: the edges, tones and shifts left Resonators' More, which holds only
  Blend); a new `resonatorProbe` drags the Low|Red crossover and reads it back in JS and C++.
- **Overlap with T6.12** (both landed the same day): T6.12's `UI.Handle` / `UI.dragPicture` /
  `UI.Cycle` do what `UI.Proxy` / `UI.begin`+`UI.end` / `UI.Chip` do here. Proxy adds `limits()`
  (a crossover between its neighbours) and a `.proxy` class that T6.12's "proxy inside its
  `canvas.view`" check does not see. Worth folding into one widget in a polish pass.
- **Differs from the mockup:** the chips are as wide as the band column (128 px, mockup 122) so
  "root and fifth" fits; the head keeps T6.8's low-note readout and says "drag a grip for a
  crossover, a band for its level"; the Low band's Tones and Shift are shown disabled ("the root",
  "0 st") since Low has no such parameters; the keyboard strip in Harmony was left as T6.8 made it
  (it already takes the same `tel.chord.notes` and band colours).
- **For T4.2:** the live level dot beside each band's handle reads `UI.knobs[<band>_level].mod`;
  when the grid feeds those rings the dot follows without changes here.
