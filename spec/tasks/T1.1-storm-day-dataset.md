# T1.1 — Storm-day dataset (10–11 May 2024 + a quiet and a moderate day)
**Milestone:** M1   **Depends on:** —   **Branch:** feat/t1.1-storm-days

## Goal
Record the magnetosphere data the Sky Model prototype replays, and that later
becomes the bundled factory sky, the Journey presets and the test fixtures.
Verify the historical endpoints listed in `spec/02-data-sources.md`.

## Scope
- **Days** (UTC, 48 h each so a night over Alaska is always complete):
  1. `2024-05-10` → 2024-05-10T00:00Z – 2024-05-12T00:00Z: the "Gannon"
     storm (Kp 9), the reference day.
  2. A **quiet** day pair (Kp ≤ 2 throughout; e.g. an international quiet day).
  3. A **moderate** day pair with clear substorms over CMO's magnetic midnight
     (Kp 4–5, negative H bays at CMO). Pick 2 and 3 from the Kp record and
     write down why.
- **Per day, raw responses** (so parsers are tested on real formats):
  - `geomag_CMO.json`: USGS `geomag.usgs.gov/ws/data/`, `id=CMO`,
    `elements=H`, `sampling_period=60`, `starttime`/`endtime`.
  - `kp.json`: GFZ Potsdam Kp (definitive, 3-hourly) for the span.
  - `solarwind.json`: NASA OMNI 1-min (`OMNI_HRO_1MIN`; speed, density,
    Bz GSM, By GSM), e.g. via CDAWeb HAPI.
- **Per day, derived track** `track.json`: one 1-minute grid over the span,
  channels `h` (nT), `kp`, `v` (km/s), `n` (cm⁻³), `bz` (nT), `by` (nT);
  `null` for gaps (OMNI fill values → `null`); Kp stepped per 3 h interval.
  This is the prototype's input and the reference for the C++ `TrackBuilder`.
- **Manifest** `manifest.json` per day: span, source URLs, licence and credit
  line per source, retrieved-at, units, why this day.
- **Live fixtures for M5:** one current response each of SWPC
  `noaa-planetary-k-index.json`, `planetary_k_index_1m.json`,
  `plasma-1-day.json`, `mag-1-day.json` into `tests/fixtures/{kp,solarwind}/`.
- **Tool:** `tools/record_sky_day.py --day 2024-05-10 [--hours 48]`
  fetches, stores raw, builds the track, prints a sanity summary
  (max Kp, min Bz, max |ΔH| at CMO, % gaps). Python stdlib only.

## Notes
- **Day choice.** Quiet and moderate days are from January so the sky over CMO
  is dark (10 May at 65° N is twilight all night). `2025-01-19`: substorm bays
  near CMO magnetic midnight on both nights (11:15 and 11:02 UT) plus evening
  bays, waning gibbous moon. `2025-01-25`: lowest Kp window of winter 2024/25.
- **USGS `quasi-definitive`.** The default `adjusted` data had a 7 h gap on
  11 May 2024 10:23–17:30 UT (the storm's second Alaskan night);
  `quasi-definitive` is complete on all three days. It appears only ~1–3
  months after the fact, so Live (M5) uses `adjusted`/`variation` and must
  handle gaps.
- **OMNI gaps.** On 10 May 2024 there is no plasma (v, n) before 08:04 UT and
  no IMF before 07:47 UT, then several 13–33 min gaps during the storm (v, n
  39.7 % missing; Bz 22.5 %). The model's gap rule (`04-sky-model.md` §3) decides
  what that looks like; T1.2 should check the storm doesn't "switch off" in a gap.
- **SWPC changed its products** since the spec was drafted:
  `products/solar-wind/{plasma,mag}-1-day.json` are gone (404); real-time solar
  wind is `json/rtsw/rtsw_{wind,mag}_1m.json`, newest first, with several
  spacecraft interleaved (SOLAR1, IMAP, ACE; keep `active: true`).
  `noaa-planetary-k-index.json` is now a list of objects. The fixtures keep the
  newest 120 RTSW entries (~40 min), re-serialised.
- **GFZ** moved to `kp.gfz.de` (old host redirects); the response carries
  `meta.license: "CC BY 4.0"`.
- `--rebuild` re-derives `track.json` offline from the stored raw files.

## Open questions (owner)
1. **The reference storm happens in Alaskan daylight.** On 10 May 2024 the main
   phase (≈ 17 UT 10 May – 06 UT 11 May) is daytime at CMO, and May nights
   there are twilight. Daytime is stylistic in the dome, so it still works, but
   the most dramatic hours won't have a dark sky. Add a winter storm with a
   dark-sky main phase as a fourth day later?
2. **Live polling cost (M5).** The RTSW files are 1.4 MB + 2.4 MB per fetch;
   polling every 5 min would be ~1 GB/day. Proposal: fetch every 15–30 min
   and/or look for a smaller SWPC product when T5.1 starts.
3. The Python tests are not in `ctest` yet (`tests/CMakeLists.txt` is outside
   this task); wire them in with T0.3?
