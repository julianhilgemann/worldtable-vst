# T3.9 — The EQ: four bands with drive (engine)
**Milestone:** M3   **Depends on:** T3.7   **Branch:** feat/t3.9-eq-with-drive
**Status:** approved by the owner, 2026-11-13.   **Read first:** `spec/09-ui-v2.md` §3, §7, §9; `spec/05-sound-engine.md` §7, §15; mockup `#eq`, `drawEq`, `eqDb`

Owner, 2026-11-13: "instead of filter, maybe making it an EQ with bands and drives."

## Goal
One tone stage in place of the Filter and the Drive: four bands, each with its own gain and its
own drive, so a person can saturate only one part of the sound. The sky still opens the top. This
task is the engine and the parameters; the display is T6.14.

## What exists
`fx/Filter` (two TPT SVF stages; LP12, LP24, BP, HP; the cutoff glides in log frequency; the sky's
activity, slewed 2 s, opens it by up to 3 octaves × `flt_sky`) sits between the sources and
`Drive` (ADAA tanh with asymmetry), inside `dsp/Sources`, so the sky reaches it on the tick grid.
Parameters `flt_type`, `flt_cutoff`, `flt_reso`, `flt_sky`, `drive`. Telemetry `filterHz`.

## Design
- **Bands** (fixed kinds): 1 a low shelf, 2 and 3 bells, 4 a high cut at 12 dB per octave.
- **Parameters** (`parameters.yaml`, then `tools/gen_params.py`), group `eq`:
  | ID | Range | Default | |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `eq1_freq` | 30 … 400 Hz, log | 110 | low shelf corner |
  | `eq1_gain` | −12 … +12 dB | 0 | |
  | `eq2_freq`, `eq3_freq` | 80 … 3000 Hz, 500 … 12000 Hz, log | 420, 2400 | bell centres |
  | `eq2_gain`, `eq3_gain` | −12 … +12 dB | 0 | |
  | `eq2_q`, `eq3_q` | 0.3 … 6 | 1 | bell width |
  | `eq4_freq` | 200 … 20000 Hz, log | 20000 | high cut; 20000 reads "open" |
  | `eq1_drive` … `eq4_drive` | 0 … 1 | see below | drive of that band only |
  | `eq_sky` | 0 … 1 | 0.3 | how far activity opens the high cut (× 3 octaves) |
  **Removed:** `flt_type`, `flt_cutoff`, `flt_reso`, `flt_sky`, `drive`. Nothing has been
  released. State version bump; an older state loads with the EQ's defaults. `eq_sky` is a stopgap:
  T4.2 turns it into the route Activity → `eq4_freq` and removes the parameter.
- **`fx/Eq.h`** (header first): shelves, bells and the cut from TPT state-variable filters (the
  POC's `Svf.h` or `juce::dsp`), frequencies gliding in log (τ 20 ms, as the filter does), gains
  and widths ramped per sample. A band at 0 dB adds nothing.
- **Drive per band, in parallel:** for band *k* take the part of the signal that band owns
  (*bₖ*: below the corner for the shelf, a band-pass at the bell's centre and width for the bells,
  a band-pass an octave wide ending at the cut for band 4), saturate it with the existing ADAA
  tanh (pre-gain 1 + 8·drive, level-compensated), and add only the difference:
  `out = eq(x) + Σ driveₖ · (sat(bₖ) − bₖ)`. So zero drive is exactly the EQ alone, the harmonics
  come from that band only, and nothing has to sum back flat. If a better structure turns up, put
  it in the notes before building it.
- **The sky:** the high cut's frequency × 2^(3 · `eq_sky` · activity), activity slewed 2 s, on
  the tick grid as today.
- **Defaults and the default sound:** the EQ flat and the cut open reproduce today's open filter.
  Today's Drive (0.15, broadband) has no exact equal; start every band's drive at the value that
  brings a default render closest to today's in level and spectrum (`WorldtableRender --bands`),
  say which value, and give the owner a before/after render. Goldens change on purpose; the reason
  goes in this file.
- **Telemetry:** `eqCutHz` (the cut now, after the sky) replaces `filterHz`.
- **The page:** until T6.14, the tone module shows the EQ's parameters as plain knobs (the old
  filter picture goes) so the page and `UiCheck` keep working.

## What was done
- **`dsp/fx/Eq.h`** (header-only, replaces `fx/Filter.h` and `Drive.h`, both deleted; `SvfStage`
  moved in): four TPT SVF bands in series, out = m0·x + m1·band + m2·low (Simper). With
  Ω = tan(π f / fs) / tan(π f0 / fs) and A = 10^(gain/40), the response is the RBJ cookbook's exactly:
  - low shelf (f0 the midpoint, Q 1/√2): g = tan(π f0/fs)/√A, k = √2, m = (1, k(A − 1), A² − 1);
    |H|² = A²·((A − Ω²)² + AΩ²/Q²) / ((1 − AΩ²)² + AΩ²/Q²)
  - bells: g = tan(π f0/fs), k = 1/(QA), m = (1, k(A² − 1), 0); |H|² = ((1 − Ω²)² + (ΩA/Q)²) / ((1 − Ω²)² + (Ω/(AQ))²)
  - high cut (Butterworth, 12 dB/oct): g = tan(π f0/fs), k = √2, m = (0, 0, 1); |H|² = 1/(1 + Ω⁴).
    Open from 19.98 kHz (`Eq::isOpen`): the SVF then sits at 0.49 fs (flat within 0.01 dB to 20 kHz
    at 44.1 kHz and up); draw it as 0 dB.
  - the whole curve in dB is the sum of the four. `Eq::bandDb`, `Eq::responseDb` are these formulas
    in C++ (T6.14 ports them to JavaScript; the editor now sends `sampleRate` for Ω).
- **Drive per band, in parallel:** bₖ = the part of the EQ's output y the band owns (two SVF stages:
  Butterworth low-passes at the shelf's corner; band-passes at a bell's centre, each at the bell's Q;
  octave-wide band-passes centred at cut/√2 for the cut). ADAA tanh (first order, in double), pre-gain
  G = 1 + 8·drive, bias β = 0.3·drive, level-compensated to unity small-signal slope; only the nonlinear
  part is added, eₖ = (T(Gb + β) − tanh β)/(G(1 − tanh²β)) − b̄ (b̄: the same kernel taken linearly,
  so the ADAA's half-sample delay cancels), DC-blocked at 10 Hz: `out = y + Σ driveₖ·eₖ`. A band whose
  drive is 0 skips its analysis and restarts from rest as its drive glides up.
- **Glides:** every frequency (in log2), gain, width and drive glides per sample, τ 20 ms, **in
  double**, snapping onto the target: a float one-pole stalls up to ~480 ULPs short (its step drops
  under half an ULP; 0.03 % of frequency for the cut), so it never reaches the settings. (The old
  Filter had the same stall.) At rest the coefficients are exactly those of the settings.
- **Instrument / Sources:** the EQ sits where the filter was (inside `Sources`, on the tick grid);
  `Drive` is gone from the chain. The EQ's parameters **snap** in `Instrument::glideControls` (no
  0.15 s block-rate glide): the EQ's own per-sample glide smooths them, and a move then lands on the
  same samples at any block size. The sky: `eq_sky × 3 octaves × min(activity, 1)` with the activity
  slewed 2 s, as the filter (`Sources::kEqSkyOctaves`, `kSkySlewSeconds`).
- **Parameters** (76): `eq1_freq` (30–400, 110), `eq1_gain`, `eq1_drive`, `eq2_freq` (80–3000, 420),
  `eq2_gain`, `eq2_q` (0.3–6, 1), `eq2_drive`, `eq3_freq` (500–12000, 2400), `eq3_gain`, `eq3_q`,
  `eq3_drive`, `eq4_freq` (200–20000, 20000 = open), `eq4_drive`, `eq_sky` (0.3); gains ±12 dB (0),
  drives 0–1 (0). Frequencies and Q use `mid` (geometric centre) for a log-like knob. Removed:
  `flt_type`, `flt_cutoff`, `flt_reso`, `flt_sky`, `drive`. **State version 4**; a v3 state loads
  with the EQ's defaults (tested).
- **Telemetry:** `Telemetry::eqCutHz` (the cut now, after the sky and the glide, 20000 when open)
  replaces `filterHz`; the editor sends `eqCutHz` and `sampleRate`.
- **Page (interim, until T6.14):** the Filter card is the **EQ** card: a column per band, gain over
  drive (Low, Mid, High, Cut over four Drives); its **Freq** button opens a popover with the three
  frequencies, the two widths and Sky (as T3.8's Bands). The card's head shows the cut now from
  `eqCutHz` ("cut open", "cut 10.6 kHz"). Hints for the 14 parameters, value texts ("open", "still",
  "off"), typed values. `FilterView` and `DriveView` stay in `views.js`, unused, for T6.14 to
  replace (left alone to keep this PR off the regrouping's lines). `#eq` in the URL opens the popover.
- **Render tool:** `--sky` runs the EQ at its defaults in place of `Drive`; `--bands` prints 0.01 dB;
  `--bands-of x.wav` measures an existing render (to compare with one from another build).
- **Spec:** `05` overview, diagram, §7 and §15 (now the EQ); `09` §3's interim note.

## Notes / open questions
- **For the owner's ear:** the default drive per band (0, see above); whether band 4 should also offer
  24 dB per octave (it is 12, Butterworth); whether the drive should bite harder. At this instrument's
  level a drive of 1 alone is subtle (estimated from the levels: harmonics some 30 dB under the band);
  with the band's gain up (+6 dB) it moves the octave bands by about 1 dB (renders above). The spec's pre-gain (1 + 8·drive) is kept; a
  wider range (the old Drive reached ×23) is a one-constant change (`Eq::kDrivePreGain`).
- **Structure as specified**, with three decisions: bₖ is taken from the EQ's output (a band lifted
  by its gain is driven harder, as on an analog EQ with drive); the analysis filters are two stages
  (estimated: with one stage a bell leaks a tone 4.6 octaves away at −28 dB, so its products would
  sit around −30 dB on the two-tone test; not built); only the nonlinear
  part of the ADAA output is added (the linear part through the same kernel is subtracted, else the
  half-sample delay of first-order ADAA would add a linear comb, strong near the top for band 4).
- The **EQ's parameters snap** in the instrument (they glided 0.15 s per block there before, then the
  filter glided again). The per-sample τ 20 ms is then the only smoothing: no clicks in the jump tests,
  but a frequency knob jumped by a host now arrives in about 60 ms rather than 0.5 s.
- `spec/03-architecture.md` still names `fx/Filter` and `Drive` in its module map and the companion's
  description (outside this task's paths): one line each for whoever edits it next.
- The page's EQ card is interim (T6.14 draws the curve): the frequencies, widths and Sky sit in the
  Freq popover because 14 knobs do not fit the card at the v2 knob size.
- Driving all four bands costs ~4–6 % of a core (double `exp` + `log1p` per band and channel); a
  cheaper first-order ADAA (a float `logCosh` with a wider fallback) is possible if T4.2 needs room.
