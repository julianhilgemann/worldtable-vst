# T2.3 — SkyModel: the curtain and whole frames
**Milestone:** M2   **Depends on:** T2.2   **Branch:** feat/t2.3-sky-model

## Goal
Implement the `SkyModel` class reviewed in T2.1: `setTimeline`, `seek`,
`setSpeed`, `step` and `current`. `step()` runs the slow sky from T2.2 on model
time and the curtain on real time, as the sketch's `SkyModel.step()` does, and
writes a full `SkySnapshot` (apart from the moon and the magnetic zenith,
which come in T2.5). Done when a Journey through the 10 May 2024 storm matches
the goldens' node traces.

## Design
- **Line by line from `prototype/sky/model.js`** (`SkyModel` constructor,
  `seek`, `step`, `snapshot`), in `source/sky/SkyModel.cpp`.
- **Model time in whole microseconds** (`int64_t`). One tick adds
  `round(10 000 × speed)` µs, so 24× adds exactly 0.24 s every tick, as the
  sketch's integer milliseconds do. A `double` of seconds would round on every
  add at 1.7e9 s and drift by up to 0.1 s over a day. Private members may
  change without review (T2.1).
- **Seek replays at most 59 slow steps.** The target is floored to the 1 s grid
  and clamped to the track. The start state is the checkpoint at or before
  target + 1 s, so a target at hh:mm:59 starts from the next minute's
  checkpoint and replays nothing. Seek emits no events (as in the sketch).
- **setTimeline** follows the reviewed header. A first timeline, or a
  different seed or params, re-seeks the slow state to the current model time.
  The same seed and params on a new track (a Live update) keep the slow state.
  The curtain keeps moving; it is re-laid only when the node count, span,
  station or seed changes.
- **Events:** `step()` collects the slow steps' phase changes and onsets and
  the tick's whistler into a fixed buffer of `kMaxEventsPerStep`.
- **Snapshot:** written after every `step()` and `seek()`. Sun azimuth and
  elevation are refreshed whenever the slow sky stepped (once a model second).
  The moon and the magnetic zenith stay 0 until T2.5.
- **Two read-only accessors** for tests and session saving (M6):
  `slowState()` and `lastSeekSlowSteps()`. `SkyParams` gets a defaulted
  `operator==` so `setTimeline` can tell when params changed.

## Scope

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Node goldens (10 May 2024, seed 1, 24× from the track start): the
      brightness and displacement of the 8 sampled nodes match per minute
      within a stated tolerance (1e-4 and 2e-4 km; see Notes).
- [x] The slow sky inside `SkyModel` at 6× (1 h) and 96× (12 h) is
      bit-identical to the `SkyTimeline` minute states.
- [x] Seek: bit-identical to the straight run, at most 59 slow steps, round
      trip A → B → A identical, no events.
- [x] At 1440× over the whole day: at most `kMaxEventsPerStep` events per tick,
      and the onsets and phase changes are exactly the timeline's.
- [x] Freeze (speed 0): model time and slow state hold, the curtain keeps
      moving. At the track's end, model time holds.
- [x] `step()`, `seek()`, `setSpeed()` and `setTimeline()` don't allocate.
- [x] `ctest` green (44/44); the plug-in builds with no new warnings;
      pluginval strictness 8 passes.

## Notes
- **Node goldens** (24× over the whole 48 h, 720,000 ticks, 8 nodes × 2,880
  minutes): brightness max |C++ − sketch| 5.0e-5 (mean 2.5e-5), displacement
  6.1e-5 km. The goldens are rounded to 1e-4, so the curtain matches the
  sketch to the goldens' precision, as the slow sky does (T2.2).
- **Cost:** 6.5 µs per tick in Release (64 nodes, slow steps and snapshot
  included), so about 0.07 % of one core at 100 ticks/s. ADR-0007 estimated
  "well under 1 %". Debug: 40 µs per tick.
- **Test time:** the 24× Journey test takes 29 s in Debug, and `ctest` went
  from 27 s to 71 s. It runs serially; parallel test jobs in `CMakePresets.json`
  or a Release test run in CI (T0.3) would bring that down.
- **Model time in whole µs**, so 24× adds exactly 0.24 s per tick; the seek
  target and the end clamp are on the 1 s grid, as in the sketch.
- **Events vs the timeline:** at 1440× the model's phase changes and onsets are
  field-for-field the timeline's, except the first slow step's, which
  `seek()` runs silently (as the sketch does).
- **Snapshot gaps until T2.5:** the moon (azimuth, elevation, illumination,
  waxing) and the magnetic zenith are 0. The sun is refreshed whenever the slow
  sky steps (once per model second at 1×, every tick at 1440×).
- `SkyParams::operator==` is defaulted with `-Wfloat-equal` silenced locally:
  exact equality is the point there.
- New read-only accessors: `slowState()` (tests; saving a frozen sky in M6) and
  `lastSeekSlowSteps()` (diagnostic).
