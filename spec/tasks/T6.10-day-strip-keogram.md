# T6.10 — The day strip as a keogram
**Milestone:** M6   **Depends on:** T6.9   **Branch:** feat/t6.10-day-strip-keogram
**Status:** approved by the owner, 2026-11-13.   **Read first:** `spec/09-ui-v2.md` §2; mockup `#strip`, `buildKeo`, `drawStrip`, "How to read the day strip"

Owner, 2026-11-13: "the Magnetogram is a bit cryptic"; "show me the keogram the way you envision it".

## Goal
Replace the magnetogram's five coded lanes with two pictures a person can read without a legend:
a **keogram** (what auroral observatories plot from all-sky cameras: one north-to-south slice of
the sky per moment, side by side) and under it the **magnetometer** trace. It stays the Journey's
timeline: hover reads a sentence, click or drag moves the playhead.

## Design
- **Data:** `sky::DayChart` (`SkyFeed.h`, built in `SkyFeed::buildChart` from the timeline) gains
  per model minute: `eqLatitudeDeg`, `poleLatitudeDeg`, `activity`, `gain`, `unloading`,
  `stormRed`, `mltHours`, `kp`. The `day` event carries them (2 decimals), plus `stationMlat`,
  `dayShiftDeg` and `arcPosition` from `SkyParams`. `dhModel`, `coupling`, `energy`, `threshold`
  and `phase` stay in the event (the Sky block and Details use them) but leave the strip.
- **The keogram bitmap** (page, once per loaded day, cached; 5-minute columns × 64 rows, drawn
  scaled with smoothing). It is the dome's oval shader (`dome.js` `FS_OVAL`) along the north–south
  meridian, plus the arc. For column time *t* and row *j*:
  - zenith angle ζ = −90° + 180°·(j + ½)/64 (north negative), elevation e = 90° − |ζ|;
  - for a shell at height h: s = −Rₑ·sin e + √(Rₑ²·sin² e + 2·Rₑ·h + h²), θ = atan2(s·cos e,
    Rₑ + s·sin e), mlat(h) = stationMlat ± θ; use h = 115 km (green), 250 km (red), 100 km (violet);
  - shift = dayShift/2 · (1 − cos(2π·MLT/24)), night = 0.25 + 0.75·(½ + ½·cos(2π·MLT/24)),
    eq′ = eq + shift, pole′ = pole + shift, arc = eq′ + arcPosition·(pole′ − eq′);
  - band = ss(eq′−1.5, eq′+1, g)·exp(−max(0, g−eq′−1)/3)·(1 − ss(pole′−1.5, pole′+2, g)),
    bandR = ss(eq′−6, eq′, r)·(1 − ss(eq′+3, eq′+8, r)) (g, r = mlat at 115 and 250 km; ss = smoothstep);
  - dif = 0.008 + 0.035·activity·gain + 0.04·min(1.5, u), Ia = 0.08 + 0.95·activity + 0.8·min(1.5, u);
  - the arc: Gaussians around `arc` at each height, σ = max(0.55°, 0.6 × the row's latitude step)
    for green and violet, max(1.0°, …) for red;
  - G = 5·dif·band·night·slant + 0.6·Ia·arc₁₁₅; R = 5·(0.055·stormRed + 0.006)·bandR·night·slant
    + (0.22·Ia + 0.6·stormRed·night)·arc₂₅₀; V = Ia·(0.1 + 0.9·u)·arc₁₀₀;
  - slant = 1 + 2·(1 − sin e)³; horizon fade ss(0°, 7°, e); pixel = night ground + fade ·
    tone(G·green + R·red + V·violet), tone(x) = 1 − e^(−1.7x).
  The constants were tuned by eye in the mockup (`buildKeo`). Keep them together in one place with
  a comment naming `FS_OVAL`; the owner's eye tunes them from there.
- **The strip** (`views.js`: `DayStripView` replaces `MagnetogramView`), 1110 × 92 px as in the
  mockup (the Sky block sits at its right, T6.8): row words "north", "overhead", "south" and "field" in the left gutter; a dotted line at
  overhead; the measured ΔH in ink over a zero line; hour marks every 6 h, the date at midnight;
  onsets as small carets on the top edge (filled; hollow for pseudo-breakups; size by strength);
  the part still to come dimmed 50 %; the playhead a white line with a cap.
- **Reading and moving:** hover shows a cursor and puts a sentence in the hint line: `10 May 17:45
  UT, onset, Kp 7.7, field −1063 nT. Click to jump there.` A click seeks (`seek`, as today); a
  drag scrubs, seeking at most 5 times a second and once on release. The strip is a slider for the
  keyboard: arrows ± 10 min, Shift ± 1 h, `aria-valuetext` is the sentence.
- **Mock:** `mock.js` must send the new fields. `prototype/sky`'s `runSlow` returns only the arc
  latitude: step a `SkyModel` at 1440× and sample `snapshot()` every minute (`eqLat`, `poleLat`,
  `activity`, `gain`, `unloading`, `stormRed`, `mlt`, `kp`), or add those fields to `runSlow`.
