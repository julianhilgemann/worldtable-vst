# T6.1 — The v1 UI: the dome, the instrument layer and the sky
**Milestone:** M6 (pulled forward)   **Depends on:** T2.5, T3.6   **Branch:** feat/t6.1-dome-ui

Covers T6.1 (dome renderer), T6.2 (instrument layer) and T6.3 (astronomy, toggles) of
`spec/07`; T6.4 (the cost pass) stays separate, but this task already redraws on change and
caps the dome's frame rate.

## Goal
Replace the T3.6 shell (the POC's navy/yellow knob grid) with the UI spec/06 describes: the
all-sky dome as the hero, fed by the plug-in's `SkySnapshot`; an instrument layer of hairline
controls and observatory readouts in the physics palette; the magnetogram; Sound and Space FX
in tabs. Every one of the 46 parameters keeps a control.

**Why now:** the owner reviewed T3.6 on 2026-11-06 and rejected its UI ("underwhelming,
undercooked … compared to how the VST originally looked and the mockups"). The look the
owner signed off is the T1.2 sketch's dome, the Hero Concepts canvas and the POC's density of
live pictures; this task joins them. M4/M5 follow after.

## Design

### Layout (1200 × 800 base, scaled; the editor's existing size)
```
┌ header: mark · WORLDTABLE · CMO 64.87N 147.86W · date · UT · Kp · Bz · v · JOURNEY 24× · meter ┐
├ dome 460² ──────────┬ CHORD  label, roman, function, state · voicing view (pitch axis)  ─────┤
│ all-sky camera:     ├ SKY    phase track · readouts · event ticker                          │
│ curtain, oval,      ├ MACROS Storm · Breath · Glow · Space · Drift (large hairline arcs)     │
│ stars, moon, sun    │                                                                        │
├ MAGNETOGRAM  ΔH CMO vs model · coupling · phase band · onsets · now-marker (whole day) ─────┤
├ tabs: Sound · Space FX · Sky ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Sound: harmony (key row, mood, scale, shape, progression … drift) · layers (+ decay view)   │
│ Space FX: Drive → Phaser → Chorus → Delay → Reverb → Output, each with its live picture      │
│ Sky: station, Journey, view toggles, data credits                                            │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
spec/06 drew 1000 × 640 with the tabs under the magnetogram and the macros beside the dome;
the editor is already 1200 × 800 (POC, T3.6), and the extra room is what lets every FX stage
keep a live picture. spec/06's layout block is updated to this.

### Materials (spec/06 "Visual identity")
- Tokens: `--night #05070D`, `--ink #C9D3E6`, `--muted #8A96AD`, `--o-green #5CF2A0`,
  `--o-red #FF6B6B`, `--n-violet #8F7BFF`, `--day-rim`; hairlines are `--ink` at low alpha.
  No other hues. Colour jobs: green = body and live state, red = slow, storm, high altitude,
  violet = shimmer, highs, Bz.
- Organic layer: the dome (WebGL), a faint glow of the current emission mix behind it. Instrument
  layer: 1 px lines, small caps in JetBrains Mono, names in Instrument Sans, corner-tick frames
  (like a camera viewfinder) instead of boxes.
- Controls: hairline arcs (270°, 1 px track, 1.5 px value, a lit end point), neutral tick for
  bipolar values and macros; segmented rows instead of dropdowns where the choices fit (Key,
  Mood, Scale, Shape). Drag, shift = fine, wheel, double-click = default (the POC's handling).
- Fonts: Instrument Sans and JetBrains Mono, bundled (SIL OFL 1.1, licence files beside them),
  never loaded from the network. Approved with the go-ahead (2026-11-06; spec/06 named them).

### Data the page gets (editor, message thread only)
- `telemetry` (30 Hz) gains `dome`: per node `mlat, mlon, green, red, violet` (the sketch's
  mesh inputs) and the oval/sky scalars the dome shader reads (`activity, unloading, gain,
  eqLat, poleLat, dayShift, mlt, stormRed`), the sun and moon; and `breath` (0 … 1, the Breath
  cycle the sound uses) from `dsp::Telemetry`.
- `day` (once per page load and per new day): the station and dipole frame, and the whole day's
  chart from the published timeline, per model minute: ΔH measured and modelled, coupling,
  reservoir and threshold, phase; the onsets. `sky::SkyFeed` builds this `DayChart` when it
  loads a day (it already runs on the message thread and allocates there), so the editor never
  touches the audio side's bundle.
- No protected interface changes: `SkySnapshot.h` is read, not changed.

### Dome
A port of `prototype/sky/dome.js` (WebGL 1, Canvas 2D fallback) and the parts of
`observer.js`, `astro.js`, `stars.js` and `rng.js` it needs, as classic scripts in
`source/ui/` (the plug-in page has no module loader; the sketch stays the reference). Changes:
snapshot in seconds, node arrays from telemetry, palette from the tokens, Breath → a slow global
swell of the exposure, the output level → a little glow (spec/06), capped at 30 fps, paused when
hidden, calmer under `prefers-reduced-motion`. The ambient background (TP.2) is retired.

### Browser mock
The T1.2/T1.3 sketch *is* the mock backend (spec/06 "Mock first"): in a plain browser,
`mock.js` imports `prototype/sky/model.js`, `data.js` and `sound/harmony.js`, replays the
10 May 2024 storm from 17:20 at 24× on real time and posts the same `telemetry` and `day`
events the editor sends (chords from the sketch's Harmony, so the ticker reads true). Inside the
plug-in the mock is inactive and nothing is imported.

## Scope
`source/ui/*` (rewritten; `params.js` stays generated), `source/ui/fonts/*` (new),
`source/PluginEditor.*` (telemetry, `day`, MIME types), `source/sky/SkyFeed.*` (`DayChart`),
`source/dsp/SkySound.*`, `source/dsp/Instrument.*` (the Breath level in the telemetry),
`CMakeLists.txt` (UI files and fonts in the binary data), `tools/uicheck/UiCheck.cpp`,
`tests/unit/*`, `spec/06-ui.md`, `spec/07-implementation-plan.md`, this file, `renders/`.

## Out of scope
Motion and Data · OSC tabs (no M4/M7 yet), drag-to-modulate, Journey day/speed choice
(T4.4: the Sky tab shows them read-only), presets, the UI cost measurement (T6.4), Windows
(T0.4). Any change to the sound, the Sky Model or the goldens.

## Constraints
- Rule 1: nothing new on the audio thread except one relaxed atomic store per block (breath).
- Rule 3: no parameter changes; the UI uses the generated `params.js`.
- Rule 4 for visuals: every visual target glides (chord voicing, phase, glow, numbers), nothing
  flashes; onset is a bloom over seconds.
- Rule 6: no new dependency beyond the two bundled fonts (approved); no protected interface.
- Offline: no network from the page (fonts bundled, no CDN).

## Allowed paths
`source/ui/**`, `source/PluginEditor.*`, `source/sky/SkyFeed.*`, `source/dsp/SkySound.*`,
`source/dsp/Instrument.*`, `CMakeLists.txt`, `tools/uicheck/**`, `tests/**`, `spec/06-ui.md`,
`spec/07-implementation-plan.md`, `spec/tasks/T6.1-*`, `renders/`.

## Notes

### What landed
- **The page** (`source/ui/`, rewritten): `index.html` (layout above), `style.css` (the spec/06
  tokens, bundled fonts, corner-tick frames, hairline controls), `widgets.js` (arc knob with neutral
  tick and live ring, segmented rows, the 12-key row, toggles; `data-param` on every control),
  `views.js` (voicing, phase track, ticker, magnetogram, layer decay picture, one picture per FX
  stage, meters), `app.js` (wiring, the 30 fps loop, easing), `sky.js` + `dome.js` (the sketch's
  renderer, ported mechanically into classic scripts), `mock.js` (the sketch as the mock).
  `ambient.js` (TP.2) is gone; the dome replaces it.
- **Dome port:** identical maths. Fed the same snapshot, the sketch's renderer and the port give
  mean brightness 27.73 vs 27.41 (0–255) on a 460 px dome. Two plug-in changes, marked "T6.1" in
  `dome.js`: the backing store follows the editor zoom, and below 900 px the glow buffer stays at
  full resolution with blur radii scaled by the buffer, so the halo is the same share of the sky at
  any size (the half-res buffer showed steps at 460 px). Plus 10° azimuth ticks on the horizon.
- **Telemetry** (`PluginEditor`): `dome` (64 × mlat, mlon, green, red, violet, the oval, sun,
  moon) and `breath` in the 30 Hz event; `sky` gains gain, coupling, density. A `day` event
  (≈ 100 KB, once per page load and per day) carries the station frame and the `DayChart`.
  MIME types for `.ttf`/`.woff2`. `SkyFeed::buildChart` takes the timeline's state *after* each
  minute (`minutes[k + 1]`), exactly what the sketch's `runSlow` trace plots; reservoir and
  threshold in units of `SkyParams::threshold`, coupling smoothed, as in the sketch's chart.
- **Breath:** `SkySound::breathCycle()` (½ − ½cos, the value the gains already used, refactored
  without changing the arithmetic) → `dsp::Telemetry::breath`, one relaxed store per block.
- **Fonts:** Instrument Sans (variable TTF, 194 KB, google/fonts) and JetBrains Mono (variable
  WOFF2, 114 KB, JetBrains/JetBrainsMono), both SIL OFL 1.1, licences in `source/ui/fonts/`.
  No glyph subsetting, so Δ, −, ×, °, arrows and ₂⁺ render in the face; ♭/♯ fall back to the
  system font.
- **UI check:** the ambient checks are replaced by the dome's (WebGL, frames drawn, nodes, the day
  chart, station) and a binding check: every parameter in `params.js` has exactly one
  `[data-param]` control (46/46). The check window is now always brought to the front (WebKit
  throttles occluded windows and the page pauses the dome while hidden). `--soak` runs 30 s.
- **Tests:** `SkyFeed charts the loaded day from its timeline` (lengths, the minute-k+1 indexing
  against a hand-built timeline, phases, onsets, sequence) and `The telemetry carries the Breath
  cycle for the UI`.
