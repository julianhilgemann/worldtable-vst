# T3.5a — FX chain I: AutoGain, DC and the render chain
**Milestone:** M3   **Depends on:** T3.3   **Branch:** feat/t3.5a-fx-chain

## Goal
Hear the sky sound through the spec's chain instead of the stand-ins: Drive → delay →
shimmer reverb → AutoGain → DC blocker → limiter, with the units the POC already has
(`Drive`, `StereoDelay` — already tape-style with wow and saturation — `ShimmerReverb`,
`Limiter`) plus the missing **AutoGain** and the DC blocker. Phaser, Chorus/Ensemble and
the shimmer's fifth are T3.5b; the plug-in swap is T3.6.

## Design
- **`fx/AutoGain`** (port of `prototype/sky/sound/fx.js`): a 3 s RMS estimate, gain
  toward −18 dBFS RMS, clamped to ±12 dB, slewed over 10 s; no K-weighting yet
  (`spec/05` §8). Unity at start, `reset()` for a new take.
- **DC blocker:** two `DcBlocker`s at 10 Hz after the auto-gain (the POC's struct).
- **Chain** in `WorldtableRender --sky` (spec order, minus Phaser/Chorus):
  Drive (0.15) → StereoDelay (1.8 s, feedback 0.35, tone 0.5, mix 0.25, ping-pong) →
  ShimmerReverb (size 0.85, T60 9 s, damping 0.35, predelay 20 ms, shimmer 0.2 at the
  octave, modulation 0.4, mix 0.35) → AutoGain (−18 dBFS ±12, 3 s/10 s) → DC → master
  gain → Limiter (−1 dBFS, 200 ms). The render now prints the chain.
- The T3.1/T3.3 tests and goldens never run the FX chain, so they are untouched.

## Scope
`source/dsp/fx/AutoGain.h`, `tests/unit/FxTests.cpp`, `tests/CMakeLists.txt`,
`tools/render/RenderOffline.cpp`, `spec/05`, `spec/07`, this task file, `renders/`.

## Out of scope
Phaser, Chorus/Ensemble, the shimmer's fifth (T3.5b). Tape-delay rework (the existing
`StereoDelay` already has wow, tone filtering and saturated feedback). The plug-in
(T3.6). Any change to `SkySound`, `Harmony`, the bank or the goldens.

## Constraints
- AutoGain is per-sample, allocation-free, no NaN; the DC blocker is stable.
- Rule 4: no clicks — AutoGain moves over ≥ 10 s; the chain is fed continuously.
- Rule 5: the render tool is deterministic (no randomness in the chain).

## Allowed paths
source/dsp/fx/AutoGain.h, tests/unit/FxTests.cpp, tests/CMakeLists.txt,
tools/render/RenderOffline.cpp, spec/05-sound-engine.md, spec/07-implementation-plan.md,

## Notes
- **AutoGain** (`fx/AutoGain.h`) is the sketch's `fx.js` AutoGain: a 3 s RMS estimate,
  gain toward −18 dBFS RMS, clamped to ±12 dB, slewed at 10 s, unity until the first
  target. It is sample-by-sample and allocation-free. There is no K-weighting/LUFS yet
  (the spec's −18 LUFS is approximated by −18 dBFS RMS); noted in `spec/05` §8.
- **DC blocker:** two `DcBlocker`s (10 Hz, the POC struct) after the auto-gain, one per
  channel.
- **Chain** in `WorldtableRender --sky` (Debug CPU in brackets): Drive 0.15
  [0.1 %] → StereoDelay 1.8 s / fb 0.35 / tone 0.5 / mix 0.25 [0.6 %] → ShimmerReverb
  size 0.85, T60 9 s, damping 0.35, predelay 20 ms, shimmer 0.2, modulation 0.4,
  mix 0.35 [3.9 %] → AutoGain [0.1 %] → DC [0.0 %] → master gain → Limiter −1 dBFS.
  Total fx 4.8 % of real time in Debug (the release figure will be lower; not measured yet).
- **Renders** (120 s storm from 17:20, progression 1): peak −1.04 dBFS, RMS −17.4,
  max step 0.0423, no NaN, 0 resets. Held storm 60 s: peak −1.0, RMS −16.6, max step
  0.0436. Quiet day (25 Jan from 09:00, held): RMS −21.1 (was −34.1 without AutoGain),
  peak −7.6, no NaN. The auto-gain's ±12 dB cannot fully lift the quiet day to −18; that
  is the range question from T1.3 open question 5, still the owner's.
- **Tests:** `tests/unit/FxTests.cpp` — AutoGain settles −23 and −9 dBFS RMS sines to
  −18 ±1 dB within 60 s and stays within ±12 dB; the DC blocker removes a constant

  the render acceptance (the tool is not a unit).
- **Untouched:** the T3.1 and T3.3 goldens never run the FX chain and still pass; the
  plug-in still plays the POC voice (T3.6). Phaser, Chorus/Ensemble and the shimmer's
