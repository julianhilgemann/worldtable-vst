## Goal
Each effect's picture shows what the effect is really doing to the sound and is the place where
its main values are set, by dragging. Knobs per module are the mockup's set. No effect's sound
changes; two small read-only taps are added for the pictures. (The tone module's picture is T6.14.)

## Engine additions (telemetry only)
- **Source spectrum:** a second `ScopeFifo` in `dsp::Instrument` fed with the mix **before the
  tone stage** (the filter today, the EQ after T3.9; in `dsp/Sources`, mono sum), analysed in the
  editor like the output scope → `tel.spectrumIn` (same bands as `tel.spectrum`). Lock-free, no
  allocation, as the existing scope.
- **Phaser LFO:** `Phaser` exposes its left LFO phase (0 … 1) once per block through
  `Telemetry::phaserPhase` (an atomic float) → `tel.phaser`.

## The pictures (log-frequency axis 20 Hz … 20 kHz where there is one)
- **Phaser** (214 wide): the source spectrum, shaped by the tone stage's response, as an outline
  (what goes in), and the same × the phaser's response filled bright (what comes out), so the
  notches are seen eating the sound. The response is exact: each stage is a first-order all-pass
  with phase −2·atan(f / f_c); N stages give θ = N × that; wet = e^(jθ) / (1 − fb·e^(jθ));
  out = (1 − mix) + mix·wet, times the make-up gain in `fx/Phaser.h`; f_c = 200 Hz ·
  10^(0.5 + 0.5·depth·sin(2π·phase)) with the phase from `tel.phaser`. Dotted lines mark the
  notches (f_c · tan((2k + 1)·π / (2N))). On the bottom edge the sweep range as a bar with two end
  caps and a dot at f_c. Drag sideways: `ph_depth`; up and down: `ph_feedback`. Title row: Stages
  as a chip (2, 4, 6, 8). Knobs: Rate, Feed, Mix.
- **Chorus** (214 wide): the dry sound as a centre line; the band the voices swing in (± the
  depth, dotted edges, the value written in the corner); the three voices as lines scrolling
  towards a fixed "now" marker with a dot each, at `ch_rate`, their strength by `ch_mix`. Drag
  sideways: `ch_rate`. Knobs: Rate, Depth, Mix.
- **Delay:** time 0 … 4 s left to right; the dry hit, then each echo as a bar (left above the
  line, right below; ping-pong alternates), heights from `dly_fb`, fading towards dark with
  `dly_tone`. A handle on the first echo: sideways `dly_time`, up and down `dly_fb`. The time is
  written in the corner. Title row: Ping-pong. Knobs: Tone, Mix.
- **Reverb:** the tail's level over time (log time, marks at 1, 5, 20, 60 s) from `rev_decay` and
  `rev_mix`, the shimmer as a brighter layer inside it, the live reverb level (`tel.verb`) as a
  dot riding the curve. A handle on the tail: sideways `rev_decay`; the decay time is written in
  the title row. Knobs: Size, Shimmer, Mix.
- **Output:** two vertical meters with a dB scale; the ceiling as a white line that can be dragged
  (`lim_ceiling`); gain reduction shown as the gap under it; the auto-gain value in the bottom bar.
  Knob: Out.
- Every other parameter of a module stays in its More popover (T6.8). Handles follow `09` §4 and
  §5 (a focusable proxy with `data-param` per parameter; arrow keys; hints).
- All ink, no hues (`09` rule 2). The macro rings from T6.7 stay on Mix and Shimmer.

## Notes / open questions
Done 2026-11-13 (agent; light protocol per the owner). PR: see `git log` for "T6.12".

- **Taps:** `Sources::setInputTap` pushes the mono mix before the EQ into a second `ScopeFifo`
  (`Instrument::getInputScope`, same lock-free `AbstractFifo`) → `tel.spectrumIn` (120 bands, the editor's
  `Analyzer` now runs twice). `Phaser::lfoPhase()` → `Telemetry::phaserPhase` per block → `tel.phaser`.
  The page also reads T3.9's `tel.sampleRate`. Renders (default, phaser-heavy, block 333, Play + MIDI)
  byte-identical before/after; a unit test compares the output with the tap attached and detached.
- **The formula differs from this file's on purpose:** −2·atan(f / f_c) with e^{jθ}/(1 − fb·e^{jθ}) is the
  analog prototype and misses the real filter by up to 16 dB above ~5 kHz (bilinear warping, and the
  feedback returns one sample late). The page draws the filter's exact response: θ = −2N·atan(tan(πf/fs) /
  tan(πf_c/fs)), wet = e^{jθ}/(1 − fb·e^{−jω}e^{jθ}) (`Phaser::magnitude`, `UI.phaserResponse`). Unit test:
  worst 0.06 dB away from the notches; UiCheck: page vs C++ 1e-14 dB.
- **The phaser picture** draws amplitude^½ against the loudest band (a −10 dB notch takes half the height),
  the tone stage via `UI.eqDb(f, UI.eqParams(tel.eqCutHz), fs)` in `widgets.js` (the one EQ response for
  T6.14). Notch lines are refined to the exact response's minima. At Mix 1 there are no notches, only the
  feedback's peaks: that is the filter, not a bug.
- **Binding:** proxies (`[data-handle]` → `UI.Handle`, `UI.handles[id]`): `ph_depth` (the sweep bar),
  `dly_time` + `dly_fb` (both on the echo handle), `rev_decay` (the tail handle), `lim_ceiling` (the line).
  `ph_stages` is the title chip (`[data-cycle]`, 2/4/6/8). The phaser's and the delay's More popovers are
  gone (empty); Reverb More keeps Damp, Pre, Mod; Output More keeps Release. Feed and the chorus Rate keep
  their knobs; the pictures' drags move them too. Drags: `UI.dragPicture` (one gesture per parameter).
- **Screenshot:** `renders/T6.12-plugin-effects.png` (real editor, 1×, with UiCheck's residue: Reverb mix 77 %, Out −12 dB).
- **Deferred to a polish pass:** per-picture captures, mock/editor screenshot sets, UI cost, pluginval.
- **Differs from the mockup:** the phaser's spectrum is the real one (sparser than the mockup's synthetic
  hump); the chorus's x axis is time (the voices run at 1.0/1.22/1.44 × the rate as `Chorus.h` does);
  the reverb's dot is the live wet level placed where the tail has that level; drag values show in the
  picture's corner while dragging or focused.
- **For T6.14 / T4.2:** `UI.eqDb` and `UI.eqParams` are shared; views read live (macro-moved) mixes from
  `UI.knobs[id].mod.now`, so T4.2's rings flow into the pictures; live dots beside handles go in the views'
  `draw`.
