# T3.5b — FX chain II: Phaser, Chorus and the shimmer's fifth
**Milestone:** M3   **Depends on:** T3.5a   **Branch:** feat/t3.5b-fx-chain-2

## Goal
Complete the spec's FX chain (spec/05 §7): a slow stereo Phaser between Drive and the
delay, a 3-voice Chorus/Ensemble after it, and the shimmer's **fifth** beside its octave.
Wire them into `WorldtableRender --sky`, prove the shimmer really produces 880/1320 Hz
from a 440 Hz input, and check the chain stays clean.

## Design
- **`fx/Phaser`:** 4–8 first-order all-pass stages per channel, one LFO sweeping the break
  frequency 200 Hz…2 kHz, feedback (soft-limited), dry/wet. The right channel's LFO is a
  quarter cycle behind, so the notches move across the field. Settings: rate 0.05 Hz,
  depth 0.6, feedback 0.3, mix 0.4, 6 stages at the default.
- **`fx/Chorus`:** a 50 ms delay line per channel, three modulated taps at 12/17/23 ms
  with 3 ms depth and rates 0.09/0.11/0.13 Hz; the right channel's LFOs are half a cycle
  behind. Dry/wet 0.25; no feedback. Allocation only in `prepare`.
- **Shimmer fifth:** `PitchShifter` currently hard-codes the +12 st slope (`phase +=
  1/window`). Give it a `ratio` (the delay sweeps at `ratio − 1` samples per sample), keep
  the existing octave at 2 and add a second shifter at 3 (+19 st, a fifth), summed at half
  gain into the same band-limited, soft-limited shimmer path. Stable at shimmer 0.6.
  (`ratio 3` is exactly an octave + a fifth: 440 → 1320 Hz.)
- **Chain** (`WorldtableRender --sky`): Drive → **Phaser** → **Chorus** → StereoDelay →
  ShimmerReverb → AutoGain → DC → master gain → Limiter (the spec's order). The run prints
  the chain.
- **Tests:** Phaser and Chorus: finite, audible, stereo-different, bypass at mix 0.
  Shimmer: a 440 Hz sine through the reverb with shimmer 0 vs 0.6 — Goertzel energy at
  880 and 1320 Hz must be far higher with shimmer; the chain render stays clean.

## Notes
- **Phaser** (`fx/Phaser.h`): 6 first-order all-pass stages, 0.05 Hz LFO sweeping 200 Hz…2 kHz,
  feedback 0.3 (soft-limited), mix 0.4, right channel a quarter cycle behind. `mix = 0` is an
  exact bypass.
- **Chorus** (`fx/Chorus.h`): 50 ms line per channel, three taps at 12/17/23 ms with 3 ms
  depth, rates 0.09/0.11/0.13 Hz, right channel half a cycle behind, mix 0.25, no feedback.
  `mix = 0` is an exact bypass.
- **Both carry a loudness make-up.** The first storm render showed the dry/wet sums ducking
  the chain by ~6 dB (the quiet day fell from −21.1 to −27.1 dBFS RMS). The make-up is
  `1 / sqrt((1−mix)² + mix²)` for the phaser and `1 / sqrt((1−mix)² + mix²/3)` for the chorus
  (the wet is the average of three taps); after it the quiet day is −21.9 and the storm
  −17.5 dBFS RMS. This is worth remembering for any future dry/wet effect.
- **Shimmer fifth:** `PitchShifter` now has a `ratio`; the delay sweeps at `ratio − 1` samples
  per sample. The existing octave is ratio 2, the new fifth ratio 3 (+19 st), summed at half
  gain into the same band-limited, soft-limited shimmer path. The spectral test runs a 440 Hz
  sine through the reverb with modulation off: with shimmer 0.6, the 880 and 1320 Hz energies
  are > 100× the shimmer-0 case and above the absolute thresholds; with shimmer 0 the FDN is
  linear, so they are essentially absent. The POC `Engine` also uses this reverb, so its
  shimmer now includes the fifth too (its tests only check finite/peak; no golden).
- **Chain:** Drive 0.15 → Phaser 0.05/0.6/0.3/0.4, 6 stages → Chorus 0.1/3 ms/0.25 →
  StereoDelay 1.8 s/0.35/0.5/0.25 → Shimmer 9 s, shimmer 0.2, mix 0.35 → AutoGain −18 dBFS
  ±12 → DC → master gain → Limiter −1 dBFS.
- **Renders** (Debug): 120 s storm from 17:20, progression 1 → peak −1.0 dBFS, RMS −17.5,
  max step 0.0524, no NaN, 0 resets, FX 7.1 % of real time (Debug). Quiet day, held → RMS
  −21.9, peak −6.7, max step 0.0334. File:
  `renders/T3.5b-2024-05-10-1720-24x-s1-progression.wav`.
- **Tests:** `FxTests` now has 25 assertions in 4 cases: AutoGain, DC, phaser/chorus
  (level, stereo, bypass, finite), shimmer fifth (Goertzel at 440/880/1320). ctest 80/80.
- **Settings are a first pass** (the spec gives no numbers); the owner's ear decides. The
  phaser/chorus make-up and the shimmer/fifth balance are the first things to tune.
- **Untouched:** T3.1/T3.3 goldens; the plug-in still plays the POC voice (T3.6). The 10 ms
  bed-burst quirk is still open.
