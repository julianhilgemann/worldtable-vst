# T1.3 — Sound sketch in the browser
**Milestone:** M1   **Depends on:** T1.2   **Branch:** feat/t1.3-sound-sketch

## Goal
Hear the Sky Model: a resonator bank tuned to a held chord, excited by the
curtain, with a slow chord progression that follows the substorm phases
(`spec/05-sound-engine.md` §1–3, `spec/01-functional-spec.md` §4). It runs on
the T1.2 page, so sound and dome are judged together. It also renders 5-minute
Journey bounces to A/B against the reference tracks with the owner (`spec/05`
§9). Exit for M1: the owner signs off the sound direction, and N = 64 or 128 is
decided by ear and CPU.

## Design
- **One clock (ADR-0007).** `SoundEngine.process()` owns the `SkyModel` and
  steps it every `sampleRate / 100` samples, counted by a running sample counter,
  so output is bit-identical whatever the block size. In the browser the engine
  runs inside an AudioWorklet, and the page draws the dome from the snapshots the
  worklet posts: the plug-in's split, where the audio thread owns the model and
  the UI reads published snapshots. With sound off the page runs as in T1.2.
- **Pure modules** (no DOM, no Web Audio nodes) run the same in the worklet, in a
  module Worker (browser bounce) and in Node (CLI bounce, tests). They are shaped
  to port to `dsp/ResonatorBank`, `Exciter` and `Harmony` (`spec/03` module map).
- **Resonators:** complex one-pole (rotating phasor), input normalised by
  √(1−r²) so loudness doesn't depend on decay. Three per voice: red (root +
  fifth, low, T60 ~20 s), green (chord, mid, ~6 s) and violet (upper chord tones,
  high, ~2.5 s). Double-banked: a chord change retunes the silent bank and moves
  the excitation to it over ~12 s while the old bank rings out. A new change waits
  until the old bank has decayed (one red T60), so no ringing resonator ever
  changes pitch by more than the drift.
- **Exciter:** seeded white noise per node; level = bed × (0.5 + activity)
  + rising brightness + onset strike (attack 0.25 s, tail ~6 s, strength from the
  onset event, weighted toward the onset longitude).
- **Harmony:** 12-TET only. Per Mood, four chord functions: home, tension,
  awe (chromatic mediants), borrowed. Sky phase → function: quiet → home,
  growth → tension, onset → awe, recovery → borrowed. Each move happens with
  probability = Progression amount (seeded); the return home always happens. In
  long phases, a seeded "wander" swaps to another chord of the same function every
  few minutes of real time, so Live at 1× doesn't sit on one chord for hours.
  Voice-leading: chords padded to 5 slots, and the slot permutation with the least
  total semitone movement wins; each voice keeps its slot and takes the nearest
  octave in its layer's range.
- **Per node:** colour (r/g/v, which already includes brightness) → layer gain
  (slewed 2 s); displacement → ± detune cents; dome azimuth → pan (east left, like
  the dome); elevation → presence. Slow per-voice drift (seeded noise, cents).
  N = 128 means two voices per node, the second at the midpoint between neighbours,
  so the sky (and its goldens) doesn't change.
- **Stand-in FX, not the spec chain (T3.5):** FDN reverb, slow auto-gain toward
  −18 dBFS RMS (±12 dB), DC blocker, lookahead limiter at −1 dBFS. Just enough
  that the A/B isn't dry.

## Scope
- `prototype/sky/sound/harmony.js`, `bank.js`, `fx.js`, `engine.js` (pure).
- `prototype/sky/sound/worklet.js` (AudioWorkletProcessor around the engine),
  `bounce-worker.js`, `wav.js` (16-bit PCM, seeded TPDF dither), `panel.js` (UI).
- `prototype/sky/bounce.mjs`: CLI bounce to `renders/` (git-ignored), prints
  peak, max step, loudness, chord changes, onsets and the real-time factor.
- Page: SOUND block (on/off, volume, Mood, root, 64/128, bounce 5 min, current
  chord), SOUND TUNING sliders, chord changes in the event ticker, sound
  constants in the review-link hash (`s.<name>=`), audio CPU in the header.
- `model.js`: replace `structuredClone` (not available in AudioWorklet scope) with
  a flat copy of the slow state. No behaviour change; the goldens must stay
  byte-identical.

## Out of scope
Sparkles, whistler voices, Breath, macros, mod matrix, the spec's FX chain,
MIDI latch, scales beyond the chord vocabulary (M3/M4). No FM of any kind
(`spec/05` §10 stays open).

## Constraints
- No dependencies, no build step. Web Audio's AudioWorklet only; no native
  filter or convolver nodes (they wouldn't port and can't run in Node).
- Rule 4: no clicks on any change (chord, Mood, root, seek, seed, tuning).
- Rule 5: same seed + day + start + speed + sample rate → identical bounce, whatever
  the block size.

## Notes
- **What the bounces do** (seed 1, 24×, times in the file):
  | Bounce | Chords |

  | storm, 10 May 17:20–19:20 UT | start Dmaj7sus2 (growth) · 0:47 onset → B♭maj7 · 1:48 recovery → Gm add9 · 4:26 wander → B♭add9 · 4:35 onset (strength 3.2) → Badd9 |
  | moderate, 19 Jan 10:00–12:00 UT | 1:04 onset → F♯add9 · 1:52 recovery → Cadd9 · 3:46 data-forced onset → Badd9 · 4:48 recovery → B♭add9 |
  | quiet, 25 Jan 09:00–11:00 UT | 2:04 wander → D6/9 · 2:30 pseudo-breakup → Dmaj7sus2 · 3:08 recovery → Cadd9 |
  All six: no NaN, peak ≤ −1.00 dBFS, max step ≤ 0.072, no limiter clamps, no resets.
  Storm and moderate sit at −17 to −19 dBFS RMS, the quiet day at −26.
- **Changed while building** (each found in a bounce):
  - A wander tied up the second bank, so the storm's 19:08 onset got its chord
    26 s late. Sky and user changes are now urgent: they damp a still-ringing old
    bank over 3 s instead of waiting a full red T60. The onset chord now lands 3 s
    after the strike.
  - The strike excited the outgoing chord (the bed crossfades over 12 s). The
    strike now always goes to the newest bank, so it lands on the new chord.
  - Every onset picked B♭maj7: the choice was seeded by a move counter that
    repeats across days. It's now seeded by the model minute, with no repeat of
    the previous chord of the same function.
  - A quiet-day pseudo-breakup (strength 0.1) took the full chromatic-mediant move.
    Onsets below 0.3 now only add tension.
  - The quiet day sat 17 dB below the storm (−34.6 dBFS RMS). Gain now follows
    √colour; the quiet day is at −26.
  - The start chord follows the phase the sky is in; the default progression is 1.
- **Determinism across environments:** a 1-minute bounce from the browser's Worker
  and from `bounce.mjs` with the same arguments are byte-identical (SHA-256
  `aff91379…`, 19 Jan from 10:00:43.44).
- **Headless Chrome** stalls AudioWorklets in `--screenshot --timeout` mode (even
  a processor that outputs zeros never finishes). Driving it over the DevTools
  protocol with Node's built-in WebSocket works; see `prototype/sky/README.md`.
- `model.js`: `structuredClone` doesn't exist in AudioWorklet scope; the flat slow
  state is now copied with a spread. Goldens byte-identical.
- The engine reads the onset node from the model's slow state; recorded as a
  needed snapshot field in `spec/04-sky-model.md` §10.
- A spectrogram of the storm bounce shows clean partial lines, chord changes as
  overlapping old and new lines, and a faint broadband wash at onsets (the limiter
  or auto-gain reacting to the strike), far below the tones. Check by ear.
- **For the C++ port (T3.1):** keep the resonator state in double (at T60 20 s,
  1 − r ≈ 7e-6). Chord-change queueing and damping live in the engine, not in
  Harmony.

## Open questions (owner)
1. **The sound direction.** Listen to `renders/T1.3-*-n64.wav`, or open the page,
   press **sound on** and play the storm at 24×. Does the curtain read as music?
   The first pass is glassy resonator clusters in a big reverb; every constant is
   a slider (SOUND TUNING).
2. **N = 64 or 128?** Compare `-n64` with `-n128` (same sky, same chords). 128 costs
   about twice the CPU and may not fit the 5 % budget in C++ without SIMD.
3. **The final three references** for the A/B (`spec/05` §9). Suggestion, one per
   role: Biosphere — Substrata (theme), Stars of the Lid — And Their Refinement
   of the Decline (drone body), Global Communication — 76:14 (uplift and shimmer).
4. **Pace at 24×:** in a storm the chord changes every 1–3 minutes, because every
   onset and recovery moves it. Right for a 60-minute Journey, or too busy?
5. **The quiet day** is 7–9 dB quieter than the storm after auto-gain. Is calm
   meant to be quieter, or should the auto-gain range grow?
6. **The onset strike** has an 80 ms attack. Should it be softer (a swell), or is a
   felt "hit" right for the climax?
