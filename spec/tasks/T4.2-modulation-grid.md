## Goal
Every routing in the instrument is visible and editable: the five macros stop being hard-wired,
the sky's channels and a few slow movers become sources, and one grid says which source moves
which control and how far. A driven control shows it with a ring.

## Design
- **The grid replaces "16 slots, curve, via"** (`05` §6): a dense matrix of depths, sources ×
  destinations, each depth −1 … +1, 0 = no route. No curve, no via, no modulators modulating
  modulators in this task.
- **Sources** (rows, three groups):
  - Macros: Storm, Breath, Glow, Space, Drift. A macro's value is 2·(m − 0.5) ∈ [−1, 1] (Breath:
    the breath cycle 0 … 1 × its knob).
  - Sky: Activity, Energy (0 … 1 of release), Onset (an envelope: 80 ms attack, τ 6 s), Brightness
    (mean node brightness), Pulsation, Bz south (0 … 1). All slewed ≥ 2 s (`CLAUDE.md` rule 4)
    except Onset, whose shape is its slew.
  - Motion: four slow LFOs (0.0005 … 2 Hz; sine, triangle, smooth random; phase in double
    precision) and two random walks. Rate and shape per source in the drawer. Seeded; on the
    sky's tick grid, so output is block-size independent (rule 5).
- **Destinations** (columns): the parameters marked `modulatable: true` in `parameters.yaml`, plus
  the four band levels. Each gets a `mod_law` there: `mul` (levels, mixes, amounts: target = base ×
  (1 + Σ depth·source), clamped to the parameter's range), `oct` (cutoff: base × 2^(3·Σ depth·
  source)), `add` (normalised, for the rest).
- **Today's wiring becomes the default grid**, so the default patch sounds the same: Storm → the
  four levels and Progression at 0.6 (today 0.4 + 1.2·m); Glow → violet level and Shimmer at 0.5;
  Space → delay mix and reverb mix at 0.5; Drift → Detune and Drift at 0.5; Breath → the three
  colour levels (today's `breathDepth` law); Activity → Cutoff at `flt_sky`'s default (the
  `flt_sky` parameter goes: it is this route). Goldens at the default patch must not change; if a
  law cannot reproduce today's exactly, say where and why before going on.
- **Engine** (`source/dsp/mod/`, header first): `ModMatrix.h` (protected), `SlowLfo`,
  `RandomWalk`. Evaluated once per sky tick (100 Hz), every target glided to the next tick; no
  allocation, no locks. Depths live in the plug-in state (a `modulation` child, not automatable);
  they reach the audio thread through an atomically swapped table, like the tracks do.
- **Telemetry:** the value of every source (0 … 1 or −1 … 1) and the modulated value of every
  destination, 30 Hz.
- **The rings** (T6.7's `setMod`): every destination with at least one non-zero depth shows the
  range (the sum of its routes at their sources' extremes) and the live dot. Pointing at a source
  lights its destinations and the other way round. A knob's context menu gains "Modulate with ▸"
  (the sources; choosing one creates a route at +0.5) and lists its routes to clear them.
- **The drawer** (bottom bar: Modulation): opens over the lower part of the middle row, above the
  chain. Three tabs (Macros, Sky, Motion). Rows are sources with a small live meter; columns stand
  **over the control they move** (their x is taken from the control's position; where a module's
  value has no knob, from its picture). A dot's size is the depth; filled pushes up, hollow pushes
  down. Drag a dot up or down to set it, double-click to clear, arrow keys with the grid focused.
  The band-level columns carry their band's colour in the label; everything else is ink.

## Acceptance criteria (whole task)
- [ ] The default patch renders identically to before (goldens), or the difference is named and accepted by the owner.
- [ ] Every macro's routes can be changed and cleared; a new route is heard within 2 s and never clicks (`NoClicks` with depths stepped during a render).
- [ ] Same seed, data and sample rate → same output at two block sizes with LFOs and walks running.
- [ ] The ring on a driven knob shows the value the engine uses (compare telemetry with the dot).
- [ ] Saved and reopened sessions keep their grid.
- [ ] Real-time checklist ticked; 5 % CPU budget measured and written down; renders for the owner's ear.
