## Goal
Every routing in the instrument is visible and editable. The five macros stop being hard-wired;
two channels of the sky, two LFOs and one envelope join them as sources; one grid says which
source moves which control and how far; a driven control shows it.

## Design
- **The grid replaces "16 slots, curve, via"** (`05` §6): a dense matrix of depths, sources ×
  destinations, each depth −1 … +1, 0 = no route. No curve, no via, no modulators modulating
  modulators in this task.
- **Sources** (rows, three groups):
  - Macros: Storm, Breath, Glow, Space, Drift. A macro's value is 2·(m − 0.5) ∈ [−1, 1]; Breath's
    is the breath cycle (0 … 1) × its knob.
  - Sky: Activity (0 … 1, slewed ≥ 2 s), Onset (an envelope fired by each substorm onset: 80 ms
    attack, τ 6 s).
  - Motion: **LFO 1, LFO 2** (0.01 … 2 Hz; sine, triangle, smooth random, stepped random; phase
    in double precision; seeded; −1 … 1) and **one Envelope** (attack, decay, sustain, release,
    each time 10 ms … 10 s; 0 … 1). The envelope fires on a substorm onset, on a MIDI note, or
    only by hand (the Fire button); a note holds it at sustain while the key is down, an onset or
    Fire holds it for 1.2 s. All on the sky's tick grid, so output is block-size independent
    (`CLAUDE.md` rule 5).
- **New parameters:** `lfo1_rate`, `lfo1_shape`, `lfo2_rate`, `lfo2_shape`, `env_attack`,
  `env_decay`, `env_sustain`, `env_release`, `env_trigger` (Onset, Note, Fire only). Fire is a
  native function from the page, not a parameter.
- **Destinations** (columns, by module): Resonators: the four band levels, Detune, Drift, Width;
  EQ: the gains of bands 1 to 3, the cut's frequency, Drive (all four bands' drive together);
  Phaser mix; Chorus mix; Delay feedback and mix; Reverb decay, shimmer and mix; Progression.
  Each has a law in `parameters.yaml` (`mod_law`): `mul` (levels, mixes, amounts: target =
  base × Π(1 + depth·source), clamped to the modulated range), `add` (the EQ gains and the drives:
  base + Σ depth·source × half the range), `oct` (the cut: base × 2^(3·Σ depth·source)). Owner,
  2026-11-13: the product, not the sum; the levels may go to 2 (+6 dB) under modulation; a
  destination **Strike** (`excite`, Resonators, no knob, base 1, 0 … 2) carries Storm to the exciter.
- **Today's wiring becomes the default grid**, so the default patch sounds the same: Storm → Strike
  and Progression at 0.6 (today 0.4 + 1.2·m); Glow → violet level and Shimmer at 0.5;
  Space → delay mix and reverb mix at 0.5; Drift → Detune and Drift at 0.5; Breath → the three
  colour levels (today's `breathDepth` law); Activity → the cut at `eq_sky`'s default (the
  `eq_sky` parameter goes: it is this route). The LFOs and the envelope start with **no routes**
  (the mockup's LFO → Width and Envelope → Shimmer are demonstrations). Goldens at the default
  patch must not change; if a law cannot reproduce today's exactly, say where and why first.
- **Engine** (`source/dsp/mod/`, header first): `ModMatrix.h` (protected), `SlowLfo`, `Envelope`.
  Evaluated once per sky tick (100 Hz), every target glided to the next tick; no allocation, no
  locks. Depths live in the plug-in state (a `modulation` child, not automatable); they reach the
  audio thread through an atomically swapped table, like the tracks do. Every data-driven target
  still slews (`CLAUDE.md` rule 4); a depth change is heard within 2 s and never clicks.
- **Telemetry:** the value of every source, the modulated value of every destination, the LFOs'
  cycle position and the envelope's stage and progress, 30 Hz.
- **The centre block** (mockup `#motion`): the Macros panel squeezed to one row (448 × 112; knobs
  40 px; the label column says "point at one to see what it moves"), and under it the **LFO**
  panel (220 × 141: a 1 | 2 switch, four shape buttons, the wave scrolling under a fixed "now"
  marker with a dot at the value, what still comes drawn dimmer, a Rate knob; drag the picture
  sideways for the rate) and the **Envelope** panel (221 × 141: a trigger chip, a Fire button,
  the shape with three points to drag (attack; decay and sustain; release), the held stretch
  shaded, a dot running along it when it fires, the four values read out underneath). Each
  picture says in its top-left corner what the source moves ("moves Width").
- **The rings** (T6.7's `setMod`): every destination with at least one route shows its range (the
  sum of its routes at their sources' extremes) and the live dot; the number stays the set value.
  In pictures the live value is a white dot beside the handle (band levels, EQ dots). Pointing at
  a source (a macro, the LFO panel, the envelope panel) lights the knobs it moves and the hint
  line lists them ("Space: … Moves: Delay mix, Reverb mix."). A knob's context menu gains
  "Modulate with ▸" (choosing a source creates a route at +0.5) and lists its routes to clear them.
- **The grid** (bottom bar: Modulation; mockup `drawGrid`): an overlay over the dome and Harmony
  (888 × 448; give it its own compositing layer, it sits over the WebGL canvas). All sources as
  rows in their three groups, each with a small live meter under its name; all destinations as
  columns under their module's name (a module with one column has no heading). A dot's size is
  the depth; filled pushes up, hollow pushes down. Drag a dot up or down to set it, double-click
  to clear, arrow keys with the grid focused; the row and column under the pointer are lit and
  the hint line reads the route. The band-level columns carry their band's colour in the label;
  everything else is ink. Escape or the Modulation button closes it.

**Open questions for the owner**
1. **Sum or product?** As approved, `mul` sums the routes: base · (1 + Σ). A product, base · Π(1 + depth·source),
   would keep today's sound at every macro setting, not only at the default. With a product, two routes
   pushing down can never silence a target between them, and the rings stay simple (the product of each
   route's extremes). I would take the product: one line of the law plus the expectations of two tests.
2. **The levels' ceiling.** Should modulation lift a level above its knob's top? Allowing up to 2 (+6 dB)
   while the knob stays 0 … 1 keeps today's Storm. Otherwise Storm above 0.5 stops lifting red, and the
   bands' balance tilts at high Storm. The AutoGain re-levels the loudness within ~10 s either way.
3. **`mul` on a zero base.** The EQ gains (0 dB) and the drives (0) start at zero, so their routes do nothing
   until the knob moves. Keep that ("a route scales your boost or cut"), or add a third law `add` (base +
   Σ·half the range) for gains and drives?
4. **Breath points down** (−0.35, −0.35, −0.5: hollow dots), as today: the inhale dips the levels. The mockup
   drew +0.5. Is the violet difference (≤ 0.07 dB at the default knob) acceptable?
5. **Storm on the levels** (point 1 above) means its hint changes from "the strike, Progression" to "the four
   levels, Progression". OK?
6. Decided in the headers; say if any is wrong:
   - A retrigger rises from where the envelope is. The mockup restarts from 0, which clicks.
   - Every note-on fires; the release comes with the last note-off.
   - Fire fires in every mode.
   - The 1.2 s hold counts after the decay, as in the mockup.
   - The curves are the mockup's.
   - Stepped random glides 50 ms per step; a shape change crossfades over 0.25 s.
   - Onset peaks at 1 whatever its strength.
   - A depth change glides at 1 per second.
   - Routes are saved by stable keys (`macro_storm` → `red_level`), so the lists can grow.
   - Defaults: LFO 1 at 0.12 Hz sine, LFO 2 at 0.03 Hz smooth random (the mockup's); envelope A 0.1 s,
     D 0.6 s, S 0.35, R 1.5 s, on Onset.
