# T2.5 — Moon, dome projection and magnetic zenith
**Milestone:** M2   **Depends on:** T2.3   **Branch:** feat/t2.5-moon-projection

## Goal
Fill the last gaps in `SkySnapshot` and give the renderer and the sound the
projection they need (`spec/04` §4–5): the moon's position and phase, the
observer's dome projection (a point in the sky → azimuth and elevation → the
dome), field-line directions and the magnetic zenith.

## Design
- **Line by line from `prototype/sky/`**, as in T2.2 and T2.3.
- `astro::moon()` (`astro.js` `moonEquatorial` and the moon half of
  `skyBodies`): low-precision series (about 0.3°), topocentric parallax,
  illuminated fraction and waxing.
- `Observer` (`observer.js`) gains the local east-north-up frame and
  `fromMag`, `fieldUp`, `pointAt`, `azEl`, `direction` and `magneticZenith()`.
  The free function `domeXY` is the equidistant fisheye (east on the left, seen
  from below). All real-time safe: the sound will call `pointAt` → `azEl` per
  node for the pan in M3.
- `SkyModel` writes the moon into `SkySnapshot::astronomy` whenever it
  refreshes the sun (about once a model second) and the magnetic zenith into
  `SkySnapshot::observer`.

## Scope
`source/sky/Astronomy.*`, `source/sky/Observer.*`, `source/sky/SkyModel.cpp`,
their tests.

## Out of scope
Stars (the renderer rotates them by sidereal time, M6), the dome renderer and
emission-line altitudes (M6), the sound's pan (M3).

## Notes
- All values match the sketch within 1e-9 (relative, absolute near zero) at
  three times and several points, including the southern-hemisphere field
  direction and a point below the horizon on the dome.
- CMO's magnetic zenith: azimuth 202.02°, elevation 77.26°.
- The moon is refreshed with the sun, whenever the slow sky steps: once per
  model second at 1×, every tick at 1440×.
- `Observer` now holds the station's local frame; `magneticZenith()` is
  computed once in the constructor.
- With T2.5 the C++ Sky Model covers everything the sketch's model does. Left
  for M2: T2.4 (block-size determinism, hand-offs per ADR-0003, running in
  `processBlock`).
