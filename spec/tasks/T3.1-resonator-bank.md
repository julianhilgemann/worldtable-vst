# T3.1 — Resonator bank and exciter
**Milestone:** M3   **Depends on:** T1.3, T2.4   **Branch:** feat/t3.1-resonator-bank

## Goal
The curtain, heard, in C++: one resonator per node and layer, tuned to a held
chord, excited by the sky, double-banked so a chord change never moves a
ringing pitch (`spec/05-sound-engine.md` §1–2, §12). Port the T1.3 sketch
(`prototype/sky/sound/bank.js`, the exciter and per-node mapping in
`engine.js`, the voicing helpers in `harmony.js`) line by line, as M2 did for
the sky, and prove it against a sound golden exported from the sketch the
owner signed off. Measure the real C++ cost of 64 and 128 voices per layer.

## Design
- **`dsp/ResonatorBank`** (bank.js): complex one-pole resonators,
  z ← r·e^{iω}·z + a·x, output Im(z), input × √(1−r²). State and coefficients
  in double. Layout k = (bank·3 + layer)·V + voice; two banks; inactive banks
  cost nothing. Fixed capacity (2 × 3 × 128), so nothing allocates after
  construction. Seeded xorshift32 white noise per voice; NaN/inf guard.
- **`dsp/Exciter`** (engine.js exciter): bed × (0.5 + activity), rising
  brightness (smoothed 0.3 s), onset strike (attack 80 ms, tail τ 6 s,
  0.5 + 0.5 × Gaussian around the onset node, σ 12 nodes). The bed and rise
  are shared between banks by the crossfade; the strike goes to the newest bank.
- **`dsp/Voicing`** (harmony.js voicing section): `Chord` (key, degree,
  intervals), `layerSlots`, `layerRange`, `nearestNote`, `leadSlots` (the
  5-slot permutation with the least total motion, ties broken in lexicographic
  order). Moved here from T3.2 because a retune needs them; T3.2 keeps the
  vocabulary, Mood, scales, MIDI latch, Transpose and Register controls.
- **`dsp/SkySound`** (engine.js `tick` and `process`, minus the model, the
  harmony and the effects): per node colour → layer gain (√colour × level ×
  presence, slewed 2 s), displacement → ± 3 cents, seeded drift ± 4 cents,
  dome azimuth → equal-power pan (east left), elevation → presence (10 Hz
  geometry, slewed 0.5 s). Chord requests: the new chord is voice-led into the
  silent bank and the bed moves over to it in 12 s (equal power) while the old
  bank rings out; a request waits while the old bank still rings, unless it is
  urgent (sky, user), which damps the old bank to T60 3 s instead. A newer
  request replaces a pending one. The queueing lives here, not in Harmony
  (T1.3 notes).
- **Clock (ADR-0007):** `SkyRuntime::process` gains an optional
  `TickListener`: after each model step it gets the model, the tick's events
  and the tick's length in samples, then renders the samples up to the next
  tick. `SkySound::process (runtime, left, right, n)` drives it, so the sound
  sees every tick at its sample, whatever the block size. `SkyModel` gains a
  const `observer()` accessor for the pan geometry.
- **Sound golden:** `prototype/sky/export-sound-golden.mjs` runs the sketch's
  `SoundEngine` with its effects bypassed (the exporter repeats `process()`
  without them; `engine.js` is unchanged) on the 10 May 2024 storm from
  17:20 UT at 24×, seed 1, 48 kHz, 120 s. It writes the chord requests with
  their ticks, the voiced notes of each chord, the dry RMS per 100 ms and raw
  sample windows to `tests/golden/sound/`. The C++ test replays the same
  requests at the same ticks.
- **Render:** `WorldtableRender --sky` plays the sky sound: SkyRuntime +
  SkySound, one held chord (D maj7, as the sketch with `--s.progression 0`),
  through the existing ShimmerReverb (shimmer off) and Limiter as stand-ins
  until T3.5. Prints peak, RMS, max step and CPU.

## Scope
The files above, `CMakeLists.txt` (sources), `tests/unit/SkySoundTests.cpp`,
the golden and its exporter, the render tool, spec updates.

## Out of scope
The plug-in's sound: it keeps the POC voice until the sky sound has its
effects and parameters (T3.5, T3.6). Chord vocabulary, Mood and progression
(T3.2, T3.3). Sparkles, whistlers, Breath (T3.4). The spec's FX chain and
AutoGain (T3.5). Parameters (T3.6). SIMD.

## Constraints
- Rule 1: `SkySound::process`, the bank, the exciter and the voicing don't
  allocate, lock or log; everything is sized at construction or `prepare`.
- Rule 4: a chord change never retunes a ringing resonator by more than the
  drift and detune; gains ramp per sample; no click on start, onset or change.
- Rule 5: same seed + sky + chord requests at the same ticks + sample rate →
  bit-identical output, whatever the block size.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Bank: a resonator rings at its tuned frequency (± 1 Hz at 440 Hz) and
      decays 30 dB in half its T60 (± 0.3 dB) at 44.1, 48 and 96 kHz; loudness
      at T60 2.5, 6 and 20 s within 1 dB; retuning a ringing resonator by a
      semitone doesn't raise the sample step; the guard resets NaN.
- [x] Voicing matches the sketch exactly: the golden's voiced notes for every
      chord (start and two voice-led changes), 3 layers × 64 voices each.
- [x] Golden: the C++ dry sound of the 120 s storm window matches the sketch:
      RMS per 100 ms within 4.8e-7 relative, sample windows within 7.5e-8,
      null-test residual 135 dB below the signal (Notes).
- [x] Double-banking: a change crossfades over its fade time and the old bank
      retires after the longest T60; an urgent change damps a ringing old bank
      (also mid-fade, ending the fade); a non-urgent one waits; a newer request
      replaces a pending one; no clicks through start, strike and changes.
- [x] Block sizes 4800, 128, 37 and random 1–2000 give bit-identical output
      through an onset strike; 44.1 and 48 kHz take a waiting chord on the same
      tick.
- [x] `SkySound::process` doesn't allocate, also through onsets and changes.
- [x] `WorldtableRender --sky`: no NaN/inf, peak ≤ −1 dBFS, max step ≤ 0.07;
      four 5-minute renders in `renders/` (Notes).
- [x] CPU (Release, this Mac): 1.4 % of a core at 64 voices per layer, 3.1 %
      at 128 (sky + sound); recorded in `spec/05` §10 and §12.
- [x] `ctest` green (64/64, about 2 min in Debug); AU, VST3 and Standalone
      build with no new warnings; pluginval strictness 8 passes; sketch tests
      unchanged (15/15).

## Notes
- **Golden** (`tests/golden/sound/2024-05-10-1720.json`, 115 KB): 120 s of
  the 10 May 2024 storm from 17:20 UT at 24×, seed 1, 48 kHz, dry. It covers
  the start chord D maj7sus2 (growth), the 17:38:48 onset at 46.92 s
  (strength 0.95, node 4) with an urgent change to B♭maj7 into the free bank,
  the D bank retiring at 78.9 s (12 s fade + 20 s ring-out), and the recovery
  at 108.63 s: Gm add9, voice-led back into bank 0. The C++ takes both changes
  on the sketch's ticks (4692, 10863) with identical notes. The match is to
  float precision: the snapshot is float, the sketch's arrays double.
- **Requests from outside** (`requestChord`) are taken on the next tick, like
  `seek` and `setSpeed` (T2.4). The golden test plays one tick per block so each
  request lands on its tick; Harmony (T3.3) will ask inside the tick, as the
  sketch does.
- **A sketch quirk, kept until T3.6:** on the tick a chord was taken, `engine.js` filled the
  bed weights with `other` from before the change, so for 10 ms the new bank got the whole
  bed and the old bank none. A new chord therefore started at roughly −13 dB (violet) to
  −22 dB (red) of its full level instead of silence. **Fixed in T3.6**, in the sketch and the
  port together, and the sound golden re-exported on purpose (see T3.6 notes): the weights
  now use the fresh counterpart, so the new bank starts at 0 and the 12 s equal-power
  crossfade runs from silence as designed.
- **The bank's `ramp()` keeps going past its target**, as in the sketch: the
  engine ramps again every tick, for exactly the tick's length. Documented in
  the header; the bank tests stop it after one sample.
- **Voicing moved here from T3.2**: a retune needs the layer slots, ranges and
  voice-leading. T3.2 keeps the vocabulary, Mood, scales, MIDI latch, Transpose
  and Register.
- **Renders** (`WorldtableRender --sky`, D maj7 held, 20 s pre-roll, POC
  ShimmerReverb with shimmer off and the Limiter as stand-ins, gain 0 dB):
  | File (`renders/T3.1-…`) | Onsets | Peak | RMS | Max step |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | `2024-05-10-1720-24x-s1-n64` (storm) | 2 | −1.0 dBFS | −16.9 dBFS | 0.070 |
  | `2024-05-10-1720-24x-s1-n128` | 2 | −1.0 dBFS | −16.9 dBFS | 0.067 |
  | `2025-01-19-1000-24x-s1-n64` (moderate) | 2 | −4.3 dBFS | −25.9 dBFS | 0.026 |
  | `2025-01-25-0900-24x-s1-n64` (quiet) | 1 | −16.0 dBFS | −34.1 dBFS | 0.007 |
  No NaN, no resets. Without the auto-gain (T3.5) the quiet day sits 17 dB
  below the storm, as the sketch did before its auto-gain (T1.3 notes). The
  stand-in reverb is not the sketch's FDN, so these don't sound exactly like the
  T1.3 bounces; the dry signal is the sketch's.
- **CPU** (Release, this Mac, 300 s storm, sky + sound): 64 per layer 1.4 % of a
  core, 128 per layer 3.1 %; the stand-in effects add 0.5 %. No SIMD.
- **Debug test time:** the golden test renders 120 s of audio at -O0 in 32 s;
  ctest went from about 72 s to about 2 min. The chord tests use a 10× faster
  T60 and fade (`fastSettings()`) to stay short.
- `SkyModel`'s private `observer` member is now `obs` (the sketch's name), so
  the new public `observer()` accessor can have the plain name.
