chord displayed in the middle on the right side etc." Then, on his own arrangement: "The data in

## Goal
Move every module to its place in the layout of `09` §3 and merge what belongs together. The sound
does not change; no parameter is added or removed. Pictures keep working as they are today; later
tasks rebuild them in place.

## Design
- **Grid:** rows 44 · 92 · 448 · 260 · 28, gaps 7, side margin 10. Row 2: day strip 1110, Sky 213.
  Row 3: left column 435 (Resonators 264 over the tone module 177), dome 448, Harmony 433. Row 4:
  Phaser 214, Chorus 214 │ the centre block 448 │ Delay 146, Reverb 175, Output 98. The dome stays
  448 × 448 and untouched.
- **Top bar:** brand, then today's Journey pill (T6.9 replaces it with the transport), the clock
  (date and UT), the meter. The Play pill moves into Harmony. Station and place are already in the
  dome's corner; Kp, Bz and wind move into the Sky block.
- **Sky block** (mockup `#sky`, 213 × 92, beside the strip): the phase name with its progress
  bar; one line of numbers (`567 km/s  Bz −15.5 S  Kp 7.7`); energy as a bar with the release
  threshold as a mark; activity as a bar; the last event in words. Each row has a hint. The
  four-phase track, the long readout row and the three-line ticker go; Details keeps the full
  numbers.
- **Resonators** (top left): today's Layers picture and knobs until T6.11; Detune, Drift and
  Width move here from Harmony. **Tone module** under it: today's Filter with Drive, titled
  "Filter", until T3.9 and T6.14 make it the EQ.
- **Harmony** (mockup `#harmony`) = today's Harmony + Chord + Play: the state line, the
  Drone | Play switch (the `play` parameter), the chord (42 px), roman numeral, the "why" line
  (`<function>, because <the sky's phase in words>`), the rule row (Quiet → home, Growth →
  tension, Onset → the awe move, Recovery → borrowed; the current one filled), a keyboard strip
  C1–C7 showing the notes sounding coloured by their band (it replaces the voicing lanes; in Play
  mode it shows held notes, as `cv-keys` does today), key, mood, scale, then Progression and
  Transpose. In Play mode Attack, Release, Velocity and Drone take the place of mood and scale.
- **Centre block** under the dome: the Macros panel. Until T4.2 it takes the whole block
  (448 × 260) with the five macros at 50 px; T4.2 squeezes it to one row of 112 px and adds the
  LFO and Envelope panels beneath.
- **Effects:** Phaser and Chorus left of the centre block, Delay, Reverb and Output right of it.
  Each module: title row, picture, one knob row. Visible knobs: Phaser Rate, Feed, Mix; Chorus
  Rate, Depth, Mix; Delay Tone, Mix; Reverb Size, Shimmer, Mix; Output Out. Every other parameter
  of a module goes into its **More** popover (`09` §5): Resonators: Register, Spread, Low octave
  (until T3.8); Tone: Cutoff, Resonance, Sky, Drive, with Type in the title row (until T3.9);
  Phaser: Depth, Stages; Delay: Time, Feedback, with Ping-pong in the title row; Reverb: Decay,
  Damping, Pre-delay, Modulation; Output: Ceiling, Release; Harmony: Chord shape.
- **Details** (Observer, Journey, Data): a small "i" chip in the bottom bar opens the same popover.
- Mind `UI.fit` and `width: 0; min-width: 0` for every canvas that becomes a flex item.
