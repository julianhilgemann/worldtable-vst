# T6.8 — UI v2 II: regroup the page
**Milestone:** M6   **Depends on:** T6.7   **Branch:** feat/t6.8-ui2-regroup
**Status:** draft, start when the owner says so.   **Read first:** `spec/09-ui-v2.md` §2, §3, §5; `spec/mockups/ui-v2.html`

Owner, 2026-11-13: "the logic of how things are clustered is off. Like harmony in top left while
chord displayed in the middle on the right side etc."

## Goal
Move every module to its place in the three-band layout of `09` §3: the cause on top (Sky, the
dome, Harmony), the sound chain in one strip underneath in signal order, the hint bar at the
bottom. Merge what belongs together. The sound does not change; no parameter is added or removed.

## Design
- **Grid:** rows 44 · 92 · 448 · 260 · 28, gaps 7, side margin 10; middle columns 435 · 448 · 433;
  chain widths 428 · 186 · 146 · 108 · 146 · 176 · 98. The dome stays 448 × 448 and untouched.
- **Top bar:** brand, then today's Play pill and Journey pill and the meter (T6.9 replaces the
  pill with the transport). The station, date, Kp, Bz and wind readouts leave the header: station
  and place are already in the dome's corner; date and time stay in the header as the clock; Kp,
  Bz and wind move into the Sky card.
- **Sky card** (mockup `#sky`): one sentence for the phase; the four-phase track with progress;
  the cause chain as rows with bars and numbers (Solar wind: speed, Bz with "south"/"north", Kp;
  Coupling in mV/m; Energy as a bar with the release threshold as a mark, value "% of release";
  Activity); the last three events in words (the ticker). All from the existing telemetry.
- **Macros card** under Sky: the five macros at 50 px with their captions (T6.7).
- **Harmony card** (mockup `#harmony`) = today's Harmony + Chord + Play: the state line, the
  Drone | Play switch (the `play` parameter; the header pill goes), the chord (42 px), roman
  numeral, "why" line (`<function>, because <the sky's phase in words>`), the rule row (Quiet →
  home, Growth → tension, Onset → the awe move, Recovery → borrowed; the current one filled), a
  keyboard strip C1–C7 showing the notes sounding coloured by their band (it replaces the voicing
  lanes; in Play mode it shows held notes, as `cv-keys` does today), key, mood, scale, then
  Progression and Transpose. In Play mode Attack, Release, Velocity and Drone take the place of
  mood and scale, as today.
- **Chain strip:** Resonators (today's Layers picture and knobs until T6.11; Detune, Drift, Width
  move here from Harmony), Filter (with Drive), Phaser, Chorus, Delay, Reverb, Output. Each module:
  title row, picture, one knob row. Visible knobs per module as in the mockup; every other
  parameter of the module goes into its **More** popover (`09` §5): Resonators: Register, Spread,
  Low octave (until T3.8); Phaser: Depth, Feedback, Stages; Chorus: Rate; Delay: Time, Feedback,
  Ping-pong stays in the title row; Reverb: Decay, Damping, Pre-delay, Modulation; Output: Ceiling,
  Release; Filter: Cutoff, Resonance, Type stays in the title row; Harmony: Chord shape.
- **Details** (Observer, Journey, Data): a small "i" chip in the bottom bar opens the same popover.
- Pictures keep working as they are (T6.11 and T6.12 rebuild them); only their boxes change.
  Mind `UI.fit` and `width: 0; min-width: 0` for every canvas that becomes a flex item.
