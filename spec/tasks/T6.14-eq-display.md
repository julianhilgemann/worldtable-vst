# T6.14 — The EQ display
**Milestone:** M6   **Depends on:** T3.9, T6.8   **Branch:** feat/t6.14-eq-display
**Status:** approved by the owner, 2026-11-13.   **Read first:** `spec/09-ui-v2.md` §2, §4, §5; mockup `#eq`, `drawEq`, `eqG`, its pointer handlers

## Goal
The tone module (left column, under the Resonators, 435 × 177) becomes one picture that is the
whole control of the EQ: the curve, a dot per band, a drive bar under each dot. No knob row. The
sound does not change.

## Design
- **Axes:** log frequency 20 Hz … 20 kHz, marks at 100, 1k, 10k (labels on the top edge); gain
  ± 12 dB around a 0 dB line. The lowest 27 px are the drive lane.
- **Behind:** the source spectrum (T6.12's `tel.spectrumIn`; until that lands, `tel.spectrum`)
  shaped by the EQ's curve, filled faintly. A soft glow rises from the drive lane under each
  driven band, stronger with more drive.
- **The curve:** as set, thin; as it is now (the sky's opening of the cut, later any modulation),
  ink-high with a faint fill to the 0 dB line. The response is computed in the page from the same
  formulas as `fx/Eq.h` (a unit test compares them).
- **Dots:** one per band, numbered 1 to 4, at its frequency and gain (the cut's dot sits on the
  −3 dB point). The selected one is drawn stronger. Drag sideways: frequency. Up and down: gain
  (not for the cut). Scroll over a bell: its width. When something moves a band, a white dot
  beside the handle shows the value now, joined to the handle by a hairline (`09` rule 3).
- **The sky's reach:** on the top edge, a bar from the cut as set to the cut at full activity,
  with a white dot at the cut now (`tel.eqCutHz`). It replaces the Sky knob: `eq_sky` is set by
  dragging the bar's right end.
- **Drive bars:** under each dot, in the drive lane, a 9 × 22 px bar; pull it up for that band's
  drive (`eq1_drive` … `eq4_drive`). The word "drive" labels the lane at its left.
- **Title row:** "EQ", then the selected band read out in mono: `band 2 bell 420 Hz −2.5 dB
  drive 45 %`. The read-out follows hover and drag.
- **Binding** (`09` §5): each dot is the proxy for its band's frequency; gain, width and drive
  have their own focusable proxies stacked on the dot and the bar (arrow keys: left and right
  frequency, up and down gain, `[` and `]` width, on the bar up and down drive). 14 parameters,
  one `data-param` each. Hints as in the mockup.
- All ink (`09` rule 2).

## Notes / open questions
Done 2026-11-13 (agent, light protocol).

**What changed**
- `#eq` is a title row (`EQ`, the read-out `#eq-say`) over one well `#eq-well` holding `#cv-eq` (407 × 127 CSS px;
  placed absolutely in the well, so its box never follows its backing store) and the 14 proxies. No knobs, no More
  popover (`#eq-more` and `#eq-note` are gone).
- `UI.EqView` (`views.js`) draws the mockup's `drawEq` with the engine's response and owns the gestures: a dot drags
  relatively (Shift fine) and sets frequency and gain in one gesture each (`UI.drag`); the cut's dot opens the cut at
  the right edge; a bar is a fader (absolute, as the mockup); the reach bar's end drags in octaves (relative, so the
  reach can pass 20 kHz while the bar stops at the edge); the wheel over a bell, or with a bell selected, sets its width
  (`UI.step`); a double-click resets a dot's gain (the cut's dot: open), a bar's drive, the sky's end. The read-out
  follows hover, drag and focus.
- The curve uses T6.12's `UI.eqDb(f, { bands, cutHz }, fs)` (`Eq::bandDb`'s formulas; the cut ≥ 19 980 Hz is flat):
  I had written one keyed by parameter ids and kept T6.12's at the merge, so there is one copy. `widgets.js` gained
  `UI.step`, `UI.drag` (one gesture per parameter for a multi-parameter drag) and `UI.sliderKey`.
- Proxies (`09` §5): a dot's frequency proxy is the Tab stop; gain and width are stacked on it (focusable, tabIndex −1);
  the bar and the sky's end are Tab stops. On any dot proxy ← → frequency, ↑ ↓ gain (the cut: frequency), `[` `]`
  width; on a bar or the sky's end the arrows; Shift, Home/End, Backspace, Enter (a field over the handle) as on a
  knob. `data-keys` names the keys that move a proxy; UiCheck's keyboard probe presses those.
- `FilterView`, `DriveView` deleted. `app.js`: `UI.eq = UI.EqView(…)`, `eq.draw(tel)`.
- Tests: `EqTests.cpp` "the page's curve matches the engine" runs `tests/unit/eq_page_curve.js` with node (it loads
  `params.js`, `bridge.js`, `widgets.js` into a bare `window`) and compares the shared `UI.eqDb` with `Eq::responseDb` at 20
  frequencies for three settings (48 and 44.1 kHz, extremes, an open cut): 60 checks, all well inside 0.5 dB (same
  closed forms). It SKIPs where `node` is not on the PATH. UiCheck: an EQ probe drags dot 2, scrolls bell 3, pulls bar
  1, drags the sky's end and presses a dot's keys, checks each value against the geometry, then reads the six values
  back from the processor; it also checks the picture is drawn. `moreCount` ≥ 6 now.

**My calls (mockup vs brief, or silent)**
1. The read-out adds `Q 1.08` for bells (the wheel needs feedback); the sky's end reads `sky +0.9 oct cut now 7.7 kHz`.
2. The reach bar's end is drawn as a small ring (a handle per `09` §4); the mockup has none. When the cut is open the
   bar and its ring are not drawn (the sky has nothing to open); `eq_sky` is then set from its proxy's keys.
3. The spectrum behind: T6.12's `tel.spectrumIn` (the mix before the EQ) × the curve now; where that tap is missing,
   `tel.spectrum` as it is (the output, which the EQ has already shaped; shaping it again would count the EQ twice).
4. The handles draw the values as set without easing (direct manipulation); what moves them (the cut now, from
   T4.2 any `extra` values passed to `draw`) comes from telemetry the engine has already smoothed.
5. "drive" is 11 px (the type floor for words in pictures), the dots' digits 10 px (mockup 10 and 9.5).

**For T4.2:** `eq.draw(tel, extra)`: `extra` maps parameter ids to their values now (`eq2_gain`, `eq1_drive`, …).
The live curve and the glow use them; a white dot with a hairline appears beside a dot whose frequency or gain is
moved (the cut's already does). Drive bars do not show a live value yet (draw it as a second fill or a tick).
