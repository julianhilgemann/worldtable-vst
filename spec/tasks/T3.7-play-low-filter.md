# T3.7 — Play mode, the low resonator and the filter
**Milestone:** M3   **Depends on:** T3.6, T6.5   **Branch:** feat/t3.7-play-low-filter

Asked for by the owner on 2026-11-09:
> "I want the plugin not just play chords that are predetermined and modulated by incoming signal
> but I want a simple "play" mode by pressing a keyboard button somewhere that enables me to play
> it regularly using either a sequencer or via MIDI keyboard or within ableton. […] the sound
> generally needs some more low end so if we can add some resonator around the low end that we
> can set up using the UI […] the filter from the prototype is not available anymore but I think a
> filter would be important in general, so it needs a filter in the signal chain."

Play mode reverses a v1 non-goal ("no melodic MIDI play beyond chord latch", spec/00, spec/01 §11).
The owner's request is the decision; ADR-0008 records it.

## Goal
Three additions to the instrument, each audible, each on the page:
1. **Play mode:** a Play button in the header. On, MIDI notes play the resonators as a polyphonic
   instrument (keyboard, sequencer, a clip in Ableton); the sky drone fades out (or stays under at
   the Drone level) and the sky keeps colouring the notes. Off, MIDI latches the drone's chord as
   before.
2. **Low:** a resonator layer under red, tuned to the bass note: the chord's root in drone mode,
   the lowest held note in Play mode, placed in the sub octave (C1–B1) or the bass octave (C2–B2).
3. **Filter:** a state-variable filter (the POC's SVF: LP12, LP24, BP, HP) between the sources and
   Drive, with the sky's activity opening it.

## Design
Signal flow (spec/05):
```
SkySound (curtain bank) × drone gain ─┐
PlayVoices (Play mode) ───────────────┼─► Filter ─► Drive ─► Phaser ─► … (unchanged)
LowResonator ─────────────────────────┘
```
- The three run on the sky's clock: `SkySound` gets a `Companion` hook, ticked after the curtain
  and rendered right after the bank for the same samples (`dsp/Sources`). So the sky's colours, pan
  and activity reach the notes, the low resonator and the filter on the tick grid, and the result
  is as block-size independent as the curtain (rule 5). In Play mode `Instrument::process` splits
  the block at each MIDI event, so notes start on their sample.
- **PlayVoices:** 24 voice slots, 16 held notes at most (the oldest held note is stolen). A note
  sounds from three points of the curtain (strands): each strand has four partials, the layers in
  small: red an octave down, green the note and its octave, violet the twelfth (+19). Complex
  one-pole resonators like the bank (T60 = the layer decays), excited by a strike at note-on
  (velocity; fades out as the attack lengthens, so a slow attack bows in) and a continuous bow
  while the key is down, through an attack/release envelope. Each strand takes its node's pan,
  presence, displacement detune and drift; the node's red/green/violet balance weights the
  partials; activity bows harder; an onset re-strikes held notes softly. Sustain pedal (CC64),
  all-notes-off and all-sound-off are honoured. Transpose applies to played notes.
- **LowResonator:** mono, two banks (crossfade on a new bass note: 4 s in drone mode, the attack in
  Play mode); per bank two resonators ±1.5 ct at the note and one an octave up (body, 0.35).
  Drone mode: excited by the bed (0.5 + activity) and the onset strike. Play mode: bowed while a
  note is held, struck by the lowest note-on, through the Play envelope.
- **Filter:** two TPT SVF stages per channel; LP12 = stage 1 low, LP24 = stage 2 (no resonance)
  on stage 1's low, BP, HP = stage 1. The type crossfades over 20 ms; cutoff glides per sample in
  log frequency (τ 20 ms) with coefficients per sample. Sky: the activity (slewed 2 s) opens the
  filter by up to 3 octaves × Sky (LP and BP up, HP down). Resonance 0 … 1 → damping 2 … 0.25
  (Q ≤ 4).
- **Mode switch:** the drone gain glides to Drone (Play) or 1 (off) over 2 s (rule 4). Entering
  Play clears the MIDI latch; leaving it releases every played note.

## UI (spec/06)
- Header: a Play button with a keyboard glyph beside the Journey pill (violet when on, like MIDI).
- Play on: the Harmony card becomes the Play card. Key, mood, scale and shape only steer the
  sky's chords, so their place takes a keyboard strip (held notes and their release, C1–C8 like
  the voicing view) and Attack, Release, Velocity, Drone. The six knobs on the right stay
  (Transpose, Detune and Drift act on played notes; Progression on the drone under them).
- Layers: four columns, Low first (ink: the ground under the emission lines), with Sub/Bass in
  the head and the low note's name; the picture draws its ring-out too.
- Drive becomes Filter and drive: type in the head, Cutoff, Reso, Sky, Drive; the picture is the
  live response over the spectrum with the drive curve beside it. Output keeps its row.
- Chord card in Play mode: the held notes' name, the pill "Play", the voicing view shows the
  played partials.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Play off: the default sound is unchanged apart from Low (on by default) and the open
      filter; SkySound goldens unchanged.
- [x] Play on: a note-on sounds within its attack, a note-off decays to silence within its
      release; 16-note chords and fast sequences stay finite, under the ceiling, without clicks.
- [x] Same MIDI at the same sample positions → the same output at block sizes 256, 512, 1000.
- [x] Mode switch, type switch, cutoff sweeps: no clicks (max step bounded).
- [x] `processBlock` allocates nothing with MIDI flowing.
- [x] Low follows the chord root (drone) and the lowest held note (Play), in its octave.
- [x] Filter: LP attenuates above cutoff, HP below, BP both; stable at full resonance.
- [x] `WorldtableRender --midi file.mid` renders Play mode; no NaN/inf, peak ≤ −1 dBFS.
- [x] UI: every new parameter has one control (UiCheck), screenshots of both modes.
- [x] Build without warnings, ctest green, pluginval 8.

## Done
- **DSP:** `dsp/PlayVoices` (the played notes), `dsp/LowResonator`, `dsp/fx/Filter` (the POC's SVF,
  two stages, type crossfade, per-sample log-cutoff glide), `dsp/Sources` (SkySound's companion:
  mixes and filters them on the tick grid; the mode glide; the drone gain). `SkySound` gained the
  `Companion` hook and read-only pan/presence accessors (its goldens are unchanged).
  `dsp::Instrument`: the new parameters, mode switching (entering Play clears the latch; leaving
  releases the notes), MIDI split per event in Play mode, `PlayState` and a Play-aware chord
  readout. `Harmony`'s note → chord rule is now `chordFromNotes()` (shared with Play's readout).
  `AutoGain` gained `hold` (Play mode glides to unity) and keeps its gain in double: a float gain
  stalled 0.05 dB short of a 2 s target.
- **Parameters** (12 new, 58 in all): `play`, `play_attack`, `play_release`, `play_velocity`,
  `play_drone`, `low_level`, `low_decay`, `low_octave`, `flt_type`, `flt_cutoff`, `flt_reso`,
  `flt_sky`. Nothing released yet, so no migration: an old session loads them at their defaults.
- **Calibration** (renders, octave bands from the new `--bands`): the storm drone's 31.5 Hz band
  goes from −41.9 to −28.1 dBFS at Low 0.5 (level with 63 and 125 Hz), −23.9 at Low 1. Play, dry,
  velocity 100: one note sustains near −27 dBFS RMS, a 4-note chord near −20, an 8-note chord near
  −17 with its strike peaking at −2.6 dBFS.
- **Render tool:** `--midi file.mid` (all tracks, the file's tempo map, at sample positions),
  `--block n`, `--bands` (octave-band levels of the mono mix).
- **UI:** the header's Play pill (keyboard glyph, violet when on); the Harmony card becomes Play
  in Play mode (keyboard strip with held notes, releases and the low note; Attack, Release,
  Velocity, Drone; the six knobs stay); Layers has four columns with Low first in ink, Sub/Bass and
  the low note in the head; Filter takes Drive's place with Drive inside it (type in the head,
  Cutoff, Reso, Sky, Drive; response over the spectrum, the knob's cutoff as a green tick, the
  drive curve beside it); the chord card names held notes in Play mode. Telemetry: `play`
  (voices), `low` (note, level), `filterHz`, chord `play`/`held`. The browser mock plays a demo
  phrase in Play mode; `#set=play:1,flt_cutoff:800` sets parameters for review screenshots.
- **Spec:** ADR-0008; spec/00 and spec/01 lose the "no melodic MIDI" non-goal; spec/03 module map;
  spec/05 §13 Play mode, §14 the low resonator, §15 the filter, the diagram and the FX table;
  spec/06 header, layout and modules; spec/07.
