# T3.6 — The instrument: SkySound + FX in the plug-in, parameters v1
**Milestone:** M3   **Depends on:** T3.1, T3.2, T3.3, T3.5b   **Branch:** feat/t3.6-instrument

## Goal
Make the v1 instrument playable in a DAW. `SkySound` and the spec's FX chain replace
`dsp::Engine` in `PluginProcessor`; `parameters.yaml` is rewritten for v1 (generated
params, IDs forever); the editor gets the chord readout. This is also where the two
deferred decisions land: the 10 ms bed burst on a chord-change tick (fixed in the sketch
and the port, golden re-exported on purpose) and MIDI-vs-progression precedence.

## Design

### `dsp::Instrument` (new, `source/dsp/Instrument.h/.cpp`)
The v1 sound engine, owning no model of its own:
```
SkyRuntime (processor) ─► SkySound ─► Drive ─► Phaser ─► Chorus ─► StereoDelay
                                   ─► ShimmerReverb ─► AutoGain ─► DC ─► master ─► Limiter ─► out
ParamPointers (APVTS atomics) ─► Controls (per block) ─► Harmony, SkySound::Settings, FX settings
MIDI ─► Harmony latch ─► SkySound::requestChord
```
- `prepare (sampleRate, maxBlock)`: runtime, `SkySound`, every FX stage, buffers; latency
  = the limiter's. Allocation only here.
- `process (buffer, midi)`: MIDI → latch/poll → `SkySound::requestChord (urgent)`; then
  `SkySound::process (runtime, …)` (which steps the model on the 100 Hz tick grid) and the
  chain in the render tool's order. Nothing allocates, locks or logs (CLAUDE.md rule 1).
- **Controls** are read once per block from `ParamPointers` and applied as one settings
  snapshot; every value that reaches the DSP is slewed or crossfaded (rule 4):
  - `Harmony`: key, mood, scale, chord shape, transposition, progression (atomic setters);
  - `SkySound::Settings`: register, spread, red/green/violet level and T60, detune, drift,
    stereo width, exciter bed/rise/strike;
  - FX: each stage's `Settings`; master gain through a 50 ms smoother; limiter ceiling
    and release. `AutoGain` is always on at its T3.5a settings (−18 dBFS RMS, ±12 dB).
- **Macros** (spec/01 §6, owner-confirmed 2026-11-05) are 0…1 with 0.5 neutral and scale
  the base values, so a macro never fights the parameter it moves:
  | Macro | Moves (first pass) |
  |---|---|
  | Storm | exciter bed/rise/strike ×(0.4 + 1.2·m); progression amount ×(0.4 + 1.2·m), clamped |
  | Breath | a 5-per-minute cycle: master gain ×(1 − 0.35·m·b) and violet level ×(1 − 0.4·m·b), b = ½ − ½cos |
  | Glow | violet level and reverb shimmer ×(0.5 + m) |
  | Space | reverb mix and delay mix ×(0.5 + m) |
  | Drift | detune and drift cents ×(0.5 + m) |
  Breath's full body (brightness/filter, the visual mirror) is T3.4; this is the first pass.
- **MIDI** (owner decision 2026-11-05): while notes are latched the held chord is
  authoritative — the sky keeps sensing (phase, onset and wander still advance the
  progression's target) but its requests are suppressed. On the last note-off the latch
  clears and the sky's current chord is requested urgently, so the progression resumes
  where the sky is then. With Progression 0 that is the UI's held chord. Chord controls
  still clear the latch (T3.2).
- **Chord readout**: a `ChordState` (fixed label/roman buffers, the applied chord's notes
  per layer, function/index, changes) is published through a `sky::TripleBuffer` for the
  editor; the audio thread writes it when the applied chord changes. The editor also keeps
  the existing peak/GR/reverb/spectrum telemetry (the analyzer moves here from `Engine`).

### `parameters.yaml` v1 (first pass; IDs permanent from the first release)
Groups and defaults, kept close to the render tool's constants (T3.5b) and the sketch:
- **harmony** `key` (C…B, D) · `mood` (Bright/Bittersweet/Nocturnal, Bright) ·
  `scale` (Major/Lydian/Penta Major/Minor/Dorian/Penta Minor, Major) ·
  `chord_shape` (Shape 1/2/3, 1) · `progression` 0…1 (1) · `transpose` −24…24 st (0) ·
  `register` −24…24 st (0) · `spread` 0.5…4 oct (2) · `detune` 0…12 ct (3) ·
  `drift` 0…20 ct (4).
- **layers** `red_level` 1 · `green_level` 0.7 · `violet_level` 0.4 · `red_decay` 20 s ·
  `green_decay` 6 s · `violet_decay` 2.5 s · `width` 0.9.
- **macros** `macro_storm`, `macro_breath`, `macro_glow`, `macro_space`, `macro_drift`,
  each 0…1, 0.5 (Breath 0.35).
- **fx** `drive` 0.15 · phaser `ph_rate` 0.05 Hz, `ph_depth` 0.6, `ph_feedback` 0.3,
  `ph_mix` 0.4, `ph_stages` 6 · chorus `ch_rate` 0.1 Hz, `ch_depth` 3 ms, `ch_mix` 0.25 ·
  delay `dly_time` 1.8 s, `dly_fb` 0.35, `dly_tone` 0.5, `dly_mix` 0.25, `dly_pingpong` on ·
  reverb `rev_size` 0.85, `rev_decay` 9 s, `rev_damp` 0.35, `rev_predelay` 20 ms,
  `rev_shimmer` 0.2, `rev_mod` 0.4, `rev_mix` 0.35.
- **output** `lim_ceiling` −1 dBFS · `lim_release` 200 ms · `master` 0 dB.
- No `sky_*` parameters yet: the fixed default Journey (10–11 May 2024, 24×) stays until
  autopilot (T4.4) and live data (M5). The raw-field wavetable layer is T3.6b.

### Retirement
`dsp::Engine`, `Voice`, `Lfo`, `Adsr`, `Svf`, `Music` and `data::WorldFeed` leave the
build; `WorldData`, `WorldSources`, `TrackBuilder`, `WavetableBuilder`, `WavetableSet` and
`TableExchange` stay (tested, unwired) because the raw-field layer (T3.6b) is built on
them. The POC UI panels (wavetable, filter, envelopes, LFOs, Euclid, notes) go; the editor
gets the v1 layout below.

### Editor (the chord readout is this task's UI deliverable; M6 does the real UI)
Header: sky readout (unchanged) + **chord**: label, roman numeral and the latched/pending
state, big enough to read at a glance. Rows: Harmony (with a voices view) and Layers;
Macros and Output (meter); Drive/Phaser, Chorus, Delay, Space. The ambient background
stays at its free-running level (the selectable levels were a POC switch). The browser
mock learns the v1 telemetry so the page can be reviewed without a DAW.

### 10 ms bed burst (T3.1/T3.3 notes)
On the tick a chord is taken the port fills the bed weights with `cur` and `other` after
`applyChord` has flipped `cur`, so both indices name the **new** bank and it receives the
whole bed for 10 ms (the old bank, still ringing, gets none). Fix in the sketch
(`engine.js`) and `SkySound.cpp`: recompute the weights after the change, so the new bank
starts at 0 and the old bank at 1 — the 12 s equal-power crossfade then runs from silence
as designed. Re-export `tests/golden/sound/2024-05-10-1720.json`; this is the one
deliberate golden change of M3 (reason recorded here and in T3.1).

## Constraints
- Rule 1: `process` and everything below it allocate nothing, lock, log or throw; all
  buffers in `prepare`. `tests/unit/AllocationCounter.h` proves it.
- Rule 2: no network in the plug-in path (the POC feed is gone; M5 adds the real one).
- Rule 4: every parameter reaches the DSP smoothed or through the double-banked chord
  change; no macro, preset or parameter change clicks.
- Rule 5: same seed + same day + same parameters + same sample rate → the same output at
  any block size. The instrument reads controls per block but only applies slewed values.
- New IDs from `parameters.yaml` only (rule 3); `tools/gen_params.py --check` green.

## Notes

### What landed
- **`dsp::Instrument`** (`source/dsp/Instrument.*`, new) owns `SkySound`, the chain
  (Drive → Phaser → Chorus → StereoDelay → ShimmerReverb → AutoGain → DC → master →
  Limiter), the 50/150 ms control glides, the macros, the MIDI latch and the telemetry.
  `PluginProcessor` swaps `dsp::Engine` for it; `processBlock` is one call plus
  `ScopedNoDenormals`. The chain runs per sample and the reverb's size/pre-delay glide per
  sample, so the block size changes nothing (test: 256/1000 vs 512, max sample difference
  0.0, `[determinism]`).
- **Parameters** (`spec/parameters.yaml`, 46 params, generated): harmony 10, layers 7,
  macros 5, drive 1, phaser 5, chorus 3, delay 5, reverb 7, output 3. The ID `register` had
  to become `register_semis` (`register` is a C++ keyword). `kStateVersion` is 2; a POC
  (v1) state loads and its retired IDs are ignored.
- **Macros** (owner-confirmed): Storm × exciter and × progression, Breath a 5/min cycle on
  the layer gains (evaluated on the sky's tick grid, so it is block-size independent), Glow
  violet + shimmer, Space reverb/delay mix, Drift detune + drift cents. All scale around a
  neutral 0.5.
- **Retired**: `dsp::Engine`, `Voice`, `Lfo`, `Adsr`, `Svf`, `Music`, `data::WorldFeed`, the
  POC parameters, the POC UI panels and the ambient-level switcher. `WorldData`,
  `WorldSources`, `TrackBuilder`, `WavetableBuilder`, `WavetableSet`, `TableExchange` stay
  (tested, not wired): the raw-field layer (T3.6b) is built on them.
- **Default Journey** (`SkyFeed::kDefaultJourneyStart`): the storm from **17:20 UT** at
  24×. Starting at 00:00 opened 43 real minutes before you heard the storm; the M3 renders
  all start at 17:20. Autopilot (T4.4) takes over the choice.
- **The 10 ms bed burst** (T3.1 quirk): `engine.js` and `SkySound.cpp` now use the fresh
  `1 - cur` for the bed weights, so the new bank starts at 0 and the old one at 1. Sketch
  and port agree again (null-test residual **135.3 dB** below the signal).
- **Golden re-export** (deliberate, the only M3 golden change): RMS windows change by at
  most 5.2e-3 in absolute sample units (window 1089, 0.5 % of full scale) after the 108.63 s
  recovery chord, where there is no onset strike to mask the burst; the peak moves
  0.77361 → 0.77375. The chord ticks and the applied voicings are byte-identical. Reason:
  the T3.1 notes' quirk, fixed in both.
- **Reverb (determinism fix):** `ShimmerReverb`'s size smoother used to advance once per
  `setSettings` call, so the sound depended on how often the host called it. Size and
  pre-delay now glide per sample; the line coefficients update on an absolute 64-sample
  grid. Side effect, in the right direction: a size setting is now actually reached (the
  old code called the smoother once and landed near 0.77 for 0.85). The quiet day is
  1.4 dB quieter than the T3.5b number because of the bigger reverb (−23.3 vs −21.9 dBFS).

  voicing/owner question.)

  sparkles (T3.4); the vocabulary question (scale filters chords vs Mood only) is still open.
- **Chord shape** only matters at Progression 0 (the hold); at 1 the sky writes.
- The **quiet day** still sits −23.3 dBFS RMS after the auto-gain; calm is intended, but the
  owner's ear decides whether the ±12 dB range should grow.
- The macro targets are a first pass (the brief's 4–6 macros were confirmed, the mappings
