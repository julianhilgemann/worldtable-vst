# T1.2 — Sky Model sketch in the browser
**Milestone:** M1   **Depends on:** T1.1   **Branch:** feat/t1.2-sky-sketch

## Goal
Build the Sky Model of `spec/04-sky-model.md` as a plain-JS sketch that
replays a T1.1 day and renders the all-sky dome over CMO, so the owner can
tune how it looks before any C++ exists. The sketch exports golden traces
that the C++ port (M2) must match.

Why a browser sketch (the brief allowed "web or Python"): the UI is web, the
dome renders interactively, and `model.js` later becomes the UI's mock
backend. Owner may veto.

## Scope
- `prototype/sky/model.js`: pure, no DOM. `SkyModel` with `reset(seed)`,
  `step()` at 100 Hz of model time, `snapshot()` matching the `SkySnapshot`
  fields in `spec/03-architecture.md`. Seeded RNG (counter-based), so the same
  seed and track give the same trace. Shape the code so it ports 1:1 to C++.
  - Reservoir and substorm phases (§2.1), curtain chain N = 64 (§2.2),
    pulsations (§2.3), events (§2.4), assimilation incl. data-forced onsets (§3).
- `prototype/sky/observer.js`: CMO → geomagnetic frame → dome projection (§4).
- `prototype/sky/astro.js`: sun, moon (with phase), ~100 bright stars (§5).
- `prototype/sky/dome.js`: WebGL (fallback Canvas 2D) rendering of the dome:
  curtain with rays, colour by emission line, corona at magnetic zenith,
  stars, moon, horizon rim; palette from `spec/06-ui.md`.
- `prototype/sky/index.html` + `panel.js`: day picker (T1.1 days), play /
  pause / scrub, speed 1× … 1440×, seed; readouts (UT, phase, Kp, Bz, v);
  charts of coupling, energy, H measured vs model; sliders for the tuning
  constants (§7), with "copy constants" to paste into the spec.
- `prototype/sky/export-goldens.mjs`: runs the model headless in Node over
  10 May 2024 and writes `tests/golden/sky/2024-05-10.json` (1-min resolution:
  phase, energy, activity, oval latitude, brightness of 8 sampled nodes,
  onset times).
- Run with `python3 -m http.server -d prototype/sky` (tracks are fetched
  from `resources/sky/`; serve from the repo root if paths need it).

## Constraints
- No dependencies, no build step: ES modules, WebGL 1.
- Plausible and beautiful beats correct; every visual target smoothed.
- `model.js` never reads wall-clock time or `Math.random()`.

## Allowed paths
prototype/sky/**, tests/golden/sky/**, spec/04-sky-model.md (tuned constants,
answers to §8), spec/tasks/T1.2-*.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] The dome replays any of the three T1.1 days at 1×–1440×, with pause, a
      click-to-jump strip chart, "next onset" and shareable review links (URL hash).
- [x] Phases are visible and labelled (panel, strip-chart band, event ticker).
      Data-forced onsets match CMO's bays: 19 Jan 11:30 and 20 Jan 11:01 UT (real
      bays 11:15 and 11:02). On the storm day 8 of 24 onsets are data-forced (7 on 11 May).
- [x] Quiet day: continuous curtain motion and 6 soft pseudo-breakups (strength
      0.1) in 48 h; brightness stays < 0.6 (tested).
- [~] A curtain overhead: the geometry is right (projected along dipole field
      lines; an overhead arc collapses to a band through magnetic zenith, and
      rays converge there), but a radiating corona needs stronger folds overhead
      than the model makes. See `spec/04-sky-model.md` §10.
- [x] Sun and moon vs JPL Horizons (airless, CMO, 3 times): sun ≤ 0.01°, moon
      ≤ 0.14°, illuminated fraction ≤ 0.4 %. Reference: `prototype/sky/test/horizons-cmo.json`.
- [x] `node --test 'prototype/sky/test/*.test.mjs'`: 8/8. Covers identical traces
      for the same seed, a different sky for a different seed, a slow sky that is
      bit-identical at 6× and 96× vs `runSlow`, seek round-trips, storm and quiet
      character, astronomy, and observer geometry.
- [x] Goldens: `tests/golden/sky/2024-05-10.json` (24×, seed 1, 470 KB), regenerated byte-identically.
- [x] **Owner has looked at it** (2026-11-05: "I like the idea. Looks interesting
      enough to keep going."). Tuned constants and answers to §8 are already
      written to `spec/04-sky-model.md` (§8–§10).

## Notes
- **Two clocks** (`spec/04-sky-model.md` §1): the slow sky runs on a fixed
  1 s model-time grid, so Journey speed changes motion, never substorm timing.
  This makes `runSlow` (48 h in ~0.2 s) usable for the chart, seeking and goldens.
- **Cost:** model step ≈ 22 µs (0.2 % of a core at 100 Hz); curtain mesh
  0.6 ms per frame; stars 0.04 ms (Node, this Mac). In headless Chrome with
  software GL a frame costs ~20 ms, almost all of it GL emulation. The page
  shows its own JS ms/frame in the header; read it on real hardware.
- **Stars:** Yale BSC5 via `brettonw/YaleBrightStarCatalog` (MIT), because
  VizieR timed out from here. 111 stars with V ≤ 3.0 and Dec ≥ −26°.
- **Look:** checked by screenshots (headless Chrome, SwiftShader) at an onset
  (19 Jan 11:33), near overhead (20 Jan 13:08–13:13), the storm night
  (11 May 08:16) and the quiet day (25 Jan 10:30). The dome shows east on the
  left (seen from below).
- `tests/golden/sky/` is new; the C++ port (T2.x) reads it.

## Open questions (owner)
1. **The look.** Open the review links in `prototype/sky/README.md`. Does the
   curtain read as aurora? Ray density, colours, glow and brightness are all
   sliders (TUNING).
2. **Great storms with one arc:** the 10 May arc stays low in the south from
   CMO. Add a second, poleward arc during storms (my suggestion), or let the arc
   follow activity?
3. **Corona:** worth stronger folding at onset so a radiating corona appears
   when the arc is overhead? It is the visual climax in the brief.
4. **Pseudo-breakups** on quiet days are an invention for "never dead". Keep them?
